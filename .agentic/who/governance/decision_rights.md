# Decision rights

| Who | May |
|---|---|
| **Owner: Ally Haire** | Ratify every ADR; promote any doc to `accepted`; mark docs `superseded` or `historical` |
| **Agents** | Draft docs; propose ADRs (status `proposed`); update [STATE.md](../../../STATE.md) at the end of each session |

- An ADR is `accepted` only with a Ratification block naming the owner, how and when (e.g. "Ratified by Ally Haire in chat, 2026-10-06").
- Agents never self-ratify and never promote a doc to `accepted`.
