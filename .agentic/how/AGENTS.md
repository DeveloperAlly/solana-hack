# how/: process

- `missions/`: goal-driven units of work with phases (`mission_slug.md`).
- `backlog/`: everything planned but not in a current mission, with its build tag.
- `templates/`: templates for recurring doc types (e.g. the ADR template).
- `sessions/`: session records (Starter stub; not used during the hackathon).
