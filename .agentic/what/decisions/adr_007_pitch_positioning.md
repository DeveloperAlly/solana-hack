# ADR-007: Pitch positioning: end to end, sold first to crypto and developer brands

## Context
- The [VC and judge review](../context/vc_judge_review_2026_10_06.md) found the superhub hard to pitch as a feature list, and recommended leading with one job: proving what's official and paying for verified community work.
- The owner disagreed that the product lacks a wedge: no competitor runs the whole loop. [Research 04](../context/research/04_brand_hub_landscape.md#3-who-is-trying-to-be-the-hub-and-what-users-complain-about) agrees ("No one covers brand strategy, voice, publishing, replies, lead finding and community together").
- Crypto impersonation scams grew 1,400% year on year in 2025 ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/crypto-scams-2026/)), and Solana brands already fund community work: Superteam Earn lists 2,730+ sponsor accounts ([Superteam Earn](https://superteam.fun/earn)).
- The build itself is for the owner's own use first, with the full phased plan ([STATE.md](../../../STATE.md)).

## Decision
1. **Differentiator:** the pitch presents Waterlily as the only product that runs the whole loop: build the brand, run it, prove what's official on Solana, and pay for verified work.
2. **First buyer in the pitch:** crypto and developer brands.
3. **Scope:** this is positioning only. Build scope, ICP for the product and the traction plan are unchanged.

## Consequences
- [Pitch deck v2](../context/pitch_deck_2026_10_06.md) opens on impersonation and paid community work, and its competition slide compares tools across Build, Run, Prove and Pay.
- The deck's traction slide uses the owner's own-use numbers (Waterlily and aDNA), not outside design partners.
- Features outside the loop (unified inbox, lead finder, AI influencer, analytics) stay in the product and out of the pitch.

## Ratification
Ratified by Ally Haire in chat, 2026-10-06 ("sell it like that... I don't agree anyone does e2e yet").
