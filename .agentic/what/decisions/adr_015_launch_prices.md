# ADR-015: Launch prices

## Context
- [ADR-010](./adr_010_component_list_and_plan_tiers.md) sets the plan tiers (Free, Solo, Team, Add-on) and leaves prices open. [ADR-014](./adr_014_referral_and_launch_promotions.md) also leaves them open.
- The pitch deck (slide 10), the [pitch script](../context/pitch_script_2026_10_12.md) and the [go-to-market plan](../context/gtm_2026_10_12.md) §6 and §7 already use the prices below. An agent drafted them. AGENTS.md reserves commercial terms like these for the owner.
- Market bands, from vendor list prices ([research 10](../context/research/10_competitor_onboarding_pricing.md) §6): solo AI voice tools charge $20–49 a month; team tools charge $165–499, and Supergrow Teams and Pressmaster Agency are both $199. On payments, Aspire charges brands 2% plus processing and Superteam Earn charges 0% (research 10 §7.3, N14).

## Decision (proposed)
| Plan | Includes (ADR-010) | Launch price |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Build your voice and brand kit; Verify | $0 |
| Solo | One brand, unlimited posts, publishing to free channels, verification | $39 a month |
| Team | Seats and approvals for team, partners and ambassadors | $199 a month |
| Payments | USDC tips, rewards and KOL payments through JamJam | 3% of each payment |
| Add-on | Video, avatars, paid X posts | By use; priced later |

## Consequences
- If ratified, the deck, script and go-to-market plan drop the "launch pricing" hedge where it no longer helps, and the forecast in go-to-market §7 stands on ratified prices.
- If changed, slide 10, the slide 10 line in the pitch script, go-to-market §6 and §7, and [ADR-014](./adr_014_referral_and_launch_promotions.md)'s "Founding 100" offer (which locks the launch price) all change to match.
- The 3% fee competes with 0% bounty boards. Its case is the public record of what was approved and paid (research 10 §7.3).
