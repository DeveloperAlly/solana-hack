# ADR-000: Story Studio (Waterlily for authors)

## Context
The Colosseum Crypto World's Fair entry needed a direction. The founder owns the Waterlily name and the 2023 Waterlily.ai concept (artists opted in and were paid when AI generated in their style) ([PRD, archived](../context/archive/prd_story_studio.md)).

## Decision
Build **Story Studio**: readers commission short stories in an opted-in writer's voice; the voice owner picks a payout mode (Paid / Charity / Split / Free), and each payment settles in one Solana transaction ([PRD, archived](../context/archive/prd_story_studio.md), [roadmap, archived](../context/archive/roadmap_story_studio.md)). The self-funding AI creator was scoped as an alternative entry ([influencer scope, archived](../context/archive/influencer_scope.md), [AI creator angles, archived](../context/archive/ai_creator_angles.md)).

## Consequences
It produced the payout-mode split design and the consent-memo pattern, which later directions reused. It was replaced by a brand-focused direction (ADR-001), recorded in PRD v2.1 ([issue #2](https://github.com/DeveloperAlly/solana-hack/issues/2)).

## Ratification
Recorded retrospectively on 2026-10-06 from the archived PRD (v2, 2026-09-28), before the ADR process existed. Superseded; no ratification needed.
