Research date: 2026-10-12. Desk research only (pricing pages, help centres and docs, changelogs, review sites, third-party reviews). No accounts were created. ### Pressmaster.ai (https://www.pressmaster.ai)
- **Category:** AI thought-leadership platform: identity capture (brand guide, voice profile, "Content Twin"), AI interviews, multi-channel publishing, agent team, newsroom and press distribution.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The product has moved well past a plain post generator: docs now describe "IdentityOS", Agent Studio with a background "Super Agent", MCP, a newsroom CMS and a press-release marketplace.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** It builds an identity layer once (a brand guide from a URL or interview, voice profiles from writing samples or an interview, and a Content Twin trained through guided interviews and uploads), and every creation tool and agent draws on it. You create posts, articles, newsletters, scripts and press releases from ideas, trends, uploads or text/voice/video interviews, then review and publish to social channels, blogs or its newsroom. An agent team can plan and run content in the background, with approval rules you set.
- **Target customer:** Founders, executives and experts building thought leadership; marketing teams; agencies (white-label); enterprises.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):** The official Quickstart is a five-step, credit-rewarded checklist (up to 4,400 bonus credits):
  1. **Connect social channels (+200 credits):** Settings > Integrations, OAuth per platform. Supported: LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Facebook, YouTube, plus blogs (WordPress, Ghost, Wix, Webflow, Blogger, Medium).
  2. **Set up identity in IdentityOS (+200 credits):** four parts:
     - **Brand Foundation:** three modes: *Advanced Discovery* (recommended guided interview, 15-30 min), *Do It Yourself* (manual, or upload materials), *Quick Mode* (paste website URL for automatic extraction "in minutes"). An older help article said a URL produces a brand guide in about 45 seconds at "80-90% accuracy" (vendor claim).
     - **Voice Profile:** paste a piece of your writing for analysis, or the same three modes (Advanced Discovery 15-30 min interview / DIY rules / Quick Mode questions). The docs recommend separate social and long-form voices.
     - **Creative Director:** upload images or design samples for visual style.
     - **Content Twin:** created automatically, deepened in step 4.
  3. **Activate the Super Agent (+1,000 credits)** in Agent Studio. It monitors your niche, plans and executes tasks in the background.
  4. **Train the Twin to "100 IQ" (+1,500 credits):** guided training interviews (Basic Interview, Identity & Values, Story & Purpose, Expertise & Knowledge, Communication & Voice, Leadership & Execution) or content uploads. The docs present a Twin IQ of 100 as the point where you no longer need to review every word.
  5. **Publish your first 7 pieces (+1,500 credits)** via Instant Creation, Brainstorm or Plan Ahead. Content lands in the Content Library to "review, approve, and publish, or schedule."
  - **Interview mechanics:** text, voice or video, switchable mid-session. Pick a topic (it generates the questions, or you agree them first), go freestyle, or start from a trend or saved idea. It has persistent memory across interviews. You chat with the results, then hand off to creation. Interviews can be shared with customers or teammates to collect input. The voice interviewer runs on Hume's EVI and has four modes (structured, adaptive, conversational, external), per Hume's case study.
  - **Time-to-first-output:** the docs claim "sign-up to live post in minutes" (vendor). One G2 reviewer published the same night after seven interviews over about two hours; another reviewer found setup complex and time-consuming.
  - **Inputs:** URL, interview (text/voice/video), writing samples, file uploads, images, social OAuth.
  - **Trial / card:** Free tier ($10 one-time credits). Paid tiers say "Start Free Trial" and "No Credit Card required". Trial length is not stated. There is a daily live onboarding call.
- **Pricing:** Source pressmaster.ai/pricing, checked 2026-10-12. Billing is per user for Starter to Leader and per account above that. Annual billing is about 25% off. Usage is metered in credits (for example, a post is about 33 credits, an article about 51, an image about 124, citations/fact-check about 30, a hook simulation about 15, per docs.pressmaster.ai/credits).
  | Tier | Monthly | Annual (per mo) | Credits/mo | Key limits/features |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Free | $0 | $0 | $10 one-time | Twin, 30-day agent schedule, trends |
  | Starter | $29/user | $21.75 | 9,000 (one section says 10,000) | Posts and articles, AI interviews, 2 publishing channels, 1 voice profile, auto citations, repurposing |
  | Pro | $59/user | $44.25 | 20,000 | + Twin training, Trendmaster, hook simulation, content feedback, 2 voices |

  - **Inconsistencies on the vendor's own pages:** the meta description lists Starter at $12 and Pro at $72. Partner also appears at $59/user in another section. The docs "Plans at a glance" table omits Pro entirely. Treat Pro and Partner as partly unverified.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **Several ways to capture voice and brand:** URL quick mode, a 15-30 minute guided interview, or DIY, each for brand and for voice, plus an ongoing Twin trained through themed interviews (docs: IdentityOS).
  - **Gamified onboarding:** a five-step checklist where each step pays out credits, and the Twin IQ score gives a visible "readiness" target (docs: Quickstart).
  - **Interview as a first-class creation mode:** voice/text/video, persistent memory, agree the questions first, shareable external interviews (docs: Interview). G2 reviewers single out the interviews for drafts that sound like them.
  - **Broad publishing:** 7 social networks, 4-6 blog CMSs, its own newsroom and paid press distribution. Multiple profiles per platform, with analytics per profile.
  - **Performance and "Narrative" analytics:** benchmarks against creators your size, a topic/format quadrant, lead scoring pushed to CRM, and a "brand narrative" view showing archetype and positioning claims with the supporting posts and confidence scores (docs: Performance/Narrative).
  - **Configurable agent approval rules:** choose which tasks need your approval, approve from in-app, Slack or email, and keep an activity log of credits and outputs (docs: Agent Control). External feedback links on drafts.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No evidence-cited brand facts: URL extraction is unverified, and an accuracy claim stands in for quote-level evidence (B2).
  - No coverage map (Evidenced / Inferred / Missing) per section. The Twin IQ score measures how much material it has, not what is backed by evidence (B3).
  - No formal approval gates with who/when/why recorded on the brand kit. Approval exists for drafts and agent tasks, not as named kit sign-offs (B5). No public kit versioning or export (B6).
  - No claims gate tied to the brand's evidence. It offers article citations and fact-checking from web sources, which is a different thing (R2). It has a "humanize" action but no explicit anti-slop pass with reversible polish (R2).
  - The default posture runs toward autonomy (Super Agent, "without reviewing every word" at Twin IQ 100), the opposite of JamJam's "human approval before anything publishes" (R3).
  - No unified inbox across Telegram/WhatsApp (R6). No disclosed AI persona registration (R8).
  - Nothing on the Prove side: no on-chain or public registration of kits, posts or official accounts, and no Verify page or Ledger (P1-P3).
  - No ambassador campaigns, FTC auto-disclosure or USDC payouts (Y1-Y3).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Offer **three entry modes per kit section** (Quick from URL / Guided interview / DIY) on one screen. JamJam's B1/B2 already combine source ingestion and interview, so make the choice visible.
  - Show a **readiness score** like Twin IQ, but make it honest: tie it to JamJam's Evidenced/Inferred/Missing coverage (B3) rather than to volume.
  - Use a **setup checklist with rewards** (credits or extra posts) for connecting channels, finishing the kit and approving the first posts.
  - Make interviews **resumable, voice or text, and shareable** with a co-founder or customer, so JamJam can collect origin stories from others.
  - The **Narrative view** (what your posts say you stand for, with the posts as evidence) maps directly onto JamJam's evidence model. JamJam could do it with registered posts as the evidence.
  - Avoid credit opacity: the most common G2 complaint is confusing credit burn.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: Partly · Run: Yes · Prove: No · Pay: No

### Supergrow (https://www.supergrow.ai)
- **Category:** LinkedIn-only AI content platform with an AI voice interviewer ("PostCast") and a voice/identity profile ("Content DNA").
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The $19 Starter plan has been dropped since mid-2026 (the current pricing page shows Pro and Teams only).
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** It learns your writing style from pasted or existing posts and builds a five-layer "Content DNA" (identity core, voice signature, content pillars, positioning, audience). PostCast is an AI interviewer ("Alex") that runs a voice/video session, produces an analysis (insights, quotes, mindset shifts, transcript) and generates three post drafts with hook selection. Teams adds pushed interviews to executives, approval workflows and publishing on members' behalf.
- **Target customer:** Solo creators and executives on LinkedIn; marketing teams activating up to 5 executives (employee/exec advocacy); some agencies.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):** (help.supergrow.ai Get Started guide; Content Writing Style; PostCast)
  1. **Connect LinkedIn:** Settings > Accounts > Add Account > approve OAuth. This enables publishing and analytics via the official API.
  2. **Create a Writing Style:** Settings > Content Writing Style > Create New > Add Posts. You must paste at least 10 posts (15-20 recommended), or pick from existing Supergrow content. Analysis takes about 30 seconds.
  3. **Content DNA:** generated from your inputs and shown as concentric layers. You can click into each layer, and regenerate or edit if it is inaccurate.
  4. **First content via PostCast:** + New Session, pick session type (topic, text-analysis, follow-up) and format (voice and video, etc.), preview the first question, then a session of about 30 minutes with a live transcript. An email arrives when the analysis is ready. Generate My Posts gives 3 drafts. Pick a hook (from hooks extracted from your talk), optionally apply Content DNA, then regenerate or "get more versions" with instructions, then edit and schedule.
  - **Time-to-first-output:** marketing claims a 10-15 min session. The help doc says about 30 min, and Kleo testers also report about 30 min (per Taplio's review). Style analysis takes about 30 seconds.
  - **Inputs:** LinkedIn OAuth, 10+ pasted posts, voice interview, repurposed YouTube/articles/PDF.
  - **Trial / card:** 7-day free trial on every plan. After the trial, the account pauses rather than charging (pricing FAQ). Whether a card is needed is not stated.
- **Pricing:** Source supergrow.ai/pricing, checked 2026-10-12. Yearly is "save up to 20%". Billed per plan, with accounts (LinkedIn profiles) bundled.
  - **Pro:** $39/mo monthly, about $31/mo annual (per magicpost.in review, Jun 2026). 1 account. Content DNA, unlimited PostCast, repurposing, scheduling, Kanban, auto first comment, carousel maker, Engage, MCP, profile analytics, weekly report.
  - **Teams:** $199/mo monthly, about $189/mo annual (magicpost.in). 5 accounts. Content DNA per member, feedback and approval workflows, org dashboard, push content and interviews to members, publish on behalf, team analytics, leaderboard, Slack.
  - **Enterprise:** custom. API, SLA, custom onboarding, invoice billing.
  - **Note:** several 2026 reviews still quote Starter $19 / Pro $39 / Teams $139 (4 accounts). Those are superseded by the live page.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **The PostCast interview is the category's best-reviewed voice-capture mechanic.** Reviewers (magicpost.in, AuthoredUp, Taplio's own competitor review) rate it highest of its features.
  - **Post-interview analysis screen:** quotes, lessons, mindset shifts, full transcript and content ideas. The interview becomes a reusable asset, not just posts.
  - **Hook-first generation:** you pick the hook from your own words before drafts are written.
  - **Team "Content Interviews":** an admin generates questions on the BEST framework (Bond/Educate/Showcase/Tell), reviews and approves them, then pushes them with optional public no-login links, with status tracking Pending > Pushed > Completed > Post Generated.
  - **Fair trial terms** (pause, no surprise charge). It positions itself as official-API, no cookie automation (AuthoredUp rated LinkedIn safety 4.5/5).
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - It learns personal style, not a brand kit built from the brand's site with cited evidence (B2, B4). There is no coverage map (B3).
  - Approval exists only on Teams, and reviewers report single-approver friction. No who/when/why gate record on the identity profile (B5). No kit versioning or export (B6).
  - No claims gate or anti-slop pass. Reviewers say output still needs editing to avoid generic LinkedIn-speak, and there is no per-draft voice-match score (R2).
  - LinkedIn only: no X (R4). No inbox (R6), lead finder beyond Engage (R7) or persona (R8).
  - Analytics are shallow per three independent reviews, with no post-level depth (R9).
  - No Prove or Pay features (P1-P3, Y1-Y3).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - **Turn the interview into an artifact:** an analysis page with quotes, ideas and transcript that feeds both the kit and future posts.
  - **Hook selection from the owner's own words** before drafting, which fits JamJam's evidence-first rule.
  - **Pushed interviews with question review and public no-login links.** This is directly reusable for JamJam's partner/ambassador seats (Y1/Y2) and for collecting origin stories (B1).
  - **"Pause, don't charge" trial ending** as a trust signal.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: Partly · Run: Yes (LinkedIn only) · Prove: No · Pay: No

### Taplio (https://taplio.com)
- **Category:** LinkedIn growth suite (AI writing, viral-post library, scheduling, engagement, analytics, lead outreach). Owned by lempire, a sibling of Tweet Hunter.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. It has added the "Ask Taplio" agent (replacing Chat Assist), an API and a LinkedIn MCP.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** On connect it imports your full LinkedIn post history, builds a readable and correctable voice profile, and surfaces daily viral posts in your niche from a 4-5M post library. Ask Taplio drafts posts and plans weeks from your past posts, analytics and niche trends, and nothing publishes until you hit publish. Higher tiers add auto-DMs, bulk connection requests and a lead database.
- **Target customer:** LinkedIn creators, founders, consultants, sales and recruiting; agencies via the team plan.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):** (intercom.help/taplio "How do I connect my account")
  1. Create an account. Two personalisation questions: your goal (ideas, AI writing, analytics, use from Claude/ChatGPT, manage clients) and how you heard about Taplio.
  2. Install **Taplio Connect** (Chrome extension, Chromium desktop only). Setup cannot finish on mobile.
  3. **Confirm the LinkedIn profile** detected in the browser ("That's me").
  4. **Automatic setup:** scans your profile and posts, calibrates the AI to your style, finds viral posts in your niche.
  5. **Plans page:** pick Starter/Growth/Pro and monthly/yearly, then start the 7-day trial (full Pro access).
  6. Optional **Adjust AI:** About you (from LinkedIn), Tone of voice, Content pillars, Audience.
  - **Time:** "a couple of minutes" (help centre). It claims about 20 min/week of batching (use-case page).
  - **Inputs:** LinkedIn session via extension, past posts imported automatically. No interview.
  - **Trial / card: conflicting.** The homepage FAQ says "No credit card to start". The help article "How the Taplio free trial works" says to add a payment method, with auto-charge on day 7. Treat it as card required at trial start per the help centre. There is a 30-day money-back guarantee.
  - **Risk:** the extension must stay logged into LinkedIn or scheduled posts fail (help centre). This is not official-API publishing.
- **Pricing:** Source taplio.com/pricing, checked 2026-10-12. Per account/seat, yearly is 25% off (annual figures from Taplio's own review post, 2026-07-23).
  - **Starter:** $39/mo ($32/mo annual). Scheduling, core analytics, viral library. **No AI writing.**
  - **Growth:** $69/mo ($49/mo annual). AI post writing (250 AI credits/mo), 500 comment credits, smart replies, team collaboration with writers.
  - **Pro:** $199/mo ($149/mo annual). Unlimited AI and comments, lead database, auto-DMs, bulk DMs, auto-connections, API, MCP, Zapier.
  - **Team/Agency:** quote. Multi-seat discount "up to 30%".
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **Zero-effort voice capture:** full post history imported on connect, refreshed daily. The voice profile (sentence length, openers, words you never use) is readable and correctable (taplio.com/ask-taplio).
  - **Performance-aware drafting:** each post is compared against your own average, so "good" is personalised.
  - **A strong inspiration engine** (5M-post viral library, daily "For you" matches).
  - **Explicit human-in-the-loop promise for the agent:** nothing goes out until you publish.
  - **API and MCP** for drafting from Claude or ChatGPT.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand kit, site ingestion, evidence or coverage map (B1-B4). Personal voice only.
  - No approval gates with audit (B5). "Collaboration with writers" is a review share, not recorded sign-off.
  - No anti-slop or claims gate. Reviews repeatedly call output generic and needing 15-30 min editing (checkthat.ai, mjunaidkhalid.com) (R2).
  - LinkedIn only (R4). Auto-DM, auto-connect and auto-reply automation contradicts JamJam's no-auto-replies stance (R5).
  - No Prove or Pay features.
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - **Import history on connect and show the voice profile as editable facts.** This maps onto JamJam's voice section; mark each trait as Evidenced (from N posts).
  - **"Compared to your own average"** as the analytics baseline (R9).
  - **Avoid:** the price-anchor trap (the $39 tier has no AI, which is the top complaint), card-on-trial ambiguity, and extension-dependent publishing. Trustpilot is about 2.1/5, dominated by billing and cancellation complaints.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Yes (LinkedIn) · Prove: No · Pay: No

### Typefully (https://typefully.com)
- **Category:** Writing-first social scheduler (X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Substack Notes; the homepage metadata now also lists Instagram) with an AI Writing Assistant.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The Writing Assistant launched 2026-02-23. It has an API, an MCP server and agent skills.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** A distraction-free editor with pixel-accurate previews, threads, a queue and calendar, cross-posting across a "social set", and team comments and permissions. The Writing Assistant learns style from writing samples you add and drafts you write, keeps a style guide you can edit, remembers rules such as "no em-dashes", and pushes back on weak hooks.
- **Target customer:** X-first writers and creators, startup teams and ghostwriters.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Sign up with email or X (the X login pre-authorises the first connection).
  2. Create a Social Set, then OAuth for X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky or Substack (support.typefully.com).
  3. Optional: add writing samples and a style guide in Settings for the Writing Assistant.
  4. Write a draft, preview, then schedule or queue.
  - **Time:** a third-party tutorial estimates 30-40 min for a full multi-network setup (futuretweets.com, 2026-10-04). Typefully itself states no time.
  - **No interview, no brand capture.** Voice comes from samples and usage.
  - **Trial / card:** a permanent free plan, plus a 14-day full-feature trial mentioned in the X/LinkedIn page FAQs. Card for the trial is unverified.
- **Pricing:** The pricing page renders prices client-side, so direct fetch shows none. Figures below are from Sonar's read of typefully.com/pricing on 2026-09-22 (meetsonar.co), consistent with saaspricehub (2026-07-28). Per "social set" (one person or brand's accounts).
  - **Free:** $0. 1 social set, 1 user, 10 posts/mo, API/MCP.
  - **Creator:** $99/yr (annual billing only, about $8.25/mo). 1,000 posts/mo, X analytics, AI writing.
  - **Business:** $18/mo per social set billed yearly, or $20 monthly. 1,500 posts/mo, teams, roles/permissions, comments, Slack, up to 50 sets, unlimited users.
  - **Enterprise:** custom.
  - Unverified directly. Other 2026 trackers show older $12.50/$19/$39 structures, so prices have moved several times this year.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **Best-in-class editor and previews** (reviews, Framer testimonial on the pricing page).
  - **Writing Assistant with persistent rules and an editable style guide.** It pushes back rather than complying blindly (changelog).
  - **Team review:** comments, @mentions, shared drafts, an "in review" calendar state, Slack pings.
  - **Cheapest serious option:** a generous free plan, API and MCP on every tier.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, kit, evidence or gates (B1-B6).
  - No claims or anti-slop gate. The assistant edits, it does not gate (R2). Approval is collaborative review, not a required sign-off before publish (R3).
  - Analytics are X only (R9). No inbox, leads or persona.
  - No Prove or Pay features.
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - **Persistent "voice rules"** the owner sets once (banned words, no em-dashes), shown in the voice editor (R1). This also feeds JamJam's no-slop pass.
  - **Pixel-accurate per-platform preview** before approval.
  - An **"In review" status on the calendar** as the visible approval queue.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Partly · Prove: No · Pay: No

### Hypefury (https://hypefury.com)
- **Category:** Social scheduler and engagement automation for creators.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live but **no longer supports X/Twitter** (pricing FAQ). It now covers Bluesky, Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok. The free plan has been discontinued.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Cross-posting scheduler with autoplugs (auto first comment), Instagram auto-DMs, an engagement builder (watched users and keywords), Tweetshots-to-Instagram and analytics. The Full plan lists "AI trained on your posts". Ghostwriters and VAs can be invited.
- **Target customer:** Solo creators and ghostwriters, formerly X-centric.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):** The current flow is not documented publicly after X support was dropped. Older pages show "login with X", no card. Current: start a 7-day trial, connect channels. The AI learns from imported post history (the Full plan's "unified content history"). **Unverified** beyond that: help-centre articles still describe the X-era flow.
  - **Trial / card:** 7-day trial, no credit card required (pricing page).
- **Pricing:** Source hypefury.com/pricing (published 2025-09-17 and still live), checked 2026-10-12.
  - **Flexible:** $6/mo per channel (1 included, +$6 per extra). Meta description says $5 yearly. Unlimited scheduling, 2 Tweetshots/day, autoplugs, IG AutoDMs, engagement builder.
  - **Full:** $19/mo. All channels, cross-posting, LinkedIn carousels, smarter analytics, follower growth, unified history, AI trained on your posts, 5 Tweetshots/day. Annual price not shown.
- **What it does well:**
  - Cheap, simple per-channel pricing.

## Segment takeaways
- **The voice-capture method has converged on interviews plus imported posts.** Pressmaster, Supergrow, Edgar, Outerview, Sona and AuthorityMax all lead with an AI interviewer, and Taplio, Typefully and Hypefury rely on post import or samples. JamJam's guided intake (B1) is table stakes in this framing. Its edge has to come from **site ingestion with quote-level evidence and a coverage map (B2/B3)**, which nobody here does. Pressmaster's URL "Quick Mode" is the closest, and it backs accuracy with a percentage claim rather than citations.
- **Pressmaster is the most complete Build-and-Run competitor.** It has three-mode brand and voice setup (URL / 15-30 min interview / DIY), a Twin readiness score, a credit-rewarded checklist, 7 social networks plus blogs, agents and narrative analytics. It is also drifting toward autonomy ("no need to review every word"), which leaves room for JamJam's "a person approves everything" plus public proof.
- **No one has a claims gate or provenance.** Anti-slop exists only as banned-pattern lists or voice scores (Outerview, VoiceMoat) or a "humanize" button (Pressmaster). Nobody checks claims against the brand's own evidence (R2), and **no competitor has any Prove (P1-P3) or Pay (Y1-Y3) capability**. Both JamJam differentiators hold in this segment.
- **Approval is mostly a team-plan upsell** (Supergrow Teams $199, Pressmaster Agency, Sona Team), and reviewers complain about single-approver friction. Recorded approvals with who/when/why on every plan (B5/R3) would stand out.
- **Pricing norms:** a solo AI tier at $29-49/mo (Pressmaster Starter $29, Supergrow Pro $39, Edgar $49, Tweet Hunter Grow $49) and $69-99 for premium solo (Taplio Growth, Kleo). Team or agency runs $165-499/mo. Credit metering is the most-cited frustration (Pressmaster, Taplio). The "cheapest tier has no AI" anchor is widely resented (Taplio, Tweet Hunter).
- **Trial norms:** 7 days is standard. Card-free trials (Pressmaster, Hypefury, Outerview free tier) and "pause instead of charge" (Supergrow) earn trust. Taplio's card-on-trial and auto-charge drives its 2.1 Trustpilot score.
- **Channel risk:** most are LinkedIn-only. Hypefury dropped X entirely, and Taplio depends on a browser extension. JamJam's X plus LinkedIn plan (R4) should use official APIs and allow for X access risk.
- **Habit loops win retention:** a weekly interview ritual (Edgar, Outerview), a 30-day challenge (Kleo), daily trend feeds (Pressmaster, Taplio). JamJam needs a post-Build habit loop for Run.
