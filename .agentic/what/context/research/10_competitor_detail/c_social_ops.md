Research date: 2026-10-12. Desk research only, with no accounts created. Prices are in USD as listed on the vendor's own page on 2026-10-12 unless another date is given. ### Hootsuite (https://www.hootsuite.com)
- **Category:** Enterprise and SMB social media management suite. In 2026 it was rebranded as "Hootsuite Social OS".
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The suite was restructured and partly renamed on 2026-06-24. Publishing became **Perch**, Inbox became **Nest**, Amplify became **Parliament** (advocacy), and listening is **Lumen by Talkwalker**. The same release added **Wisdom**, an AI agent included on every plan, and MCP connectors across Perch, Nest and Lumen.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Hootsuite schedules and publishes to the major networks, runs a shared social inbox for customer care, and adds listening, competitor benchmarking and analytics. Wisdom is a chat-style agent built on Hootsuite's social data. It surfaces trends and risks, drafts content and recommends next steps.
- **Target customer:** Social and marketing teams from SMB up to enterprise. Enterprise is sold through a demo.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Choose a plan based on the number of accounts, posting volume and team size.
  2. Sign up with email, Google, Facebook, LinkedIn or X, which lands you on the home page.
  3. On first sign-in, Hootsuite prompts you to connect social accounts and set permissions. It recommends connecting at least three.
  4. Compose and schedule posts (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube).
  5. Manage the queue in Plan (list, week or month calendar).
  6. Set up streams and boards to monitor keywords, hashtags and mentions, and to engage.
  7. Open Analytics. Demo reports with sample data are available before your own data arrives.
  - Inputs: social OAuth only. There is no brand interview and no URL ingest. Time-to-first-output is not stated.
  - Trial: 14 days on Standard, Professional and Advanced, with **no card required** and no automatic charge at the end. During the trial, posting is capped at 10-20 posts per day per organisation and bulk scheduling is off. In the week of 2026-06-29, Hootsuite dropped its "skip trial, get 25% off" offer.
  - Sources: help.hootsuite.com/s/article/get-started-hs; hootsuite.com/plans; pricingsaas.com diff 2026W27.
- **Pricing:** Per user per month, billed annually. The page does not list monthly-billing prices and says annual billing is cheaper. Checked 2026-10-12 at hootsuite.com/plans.
  - **Standard:** from $99/user/mo. 10 social accounts, AI post and image generation, Wisdom, brand and competitor monitoring (up to 5 competitors).
  - **Professional:** from $199/user/mo. Unlimited accounts, **AI smart replies**, 90-day trend forecasting, 30-day Quick Search listening, up to 20 competitors.
  - **Advanced:** $399/user/mo. Everything in Professional plus **content review and approval workflows**.
  - **Enterprise:** custom, invoiced annually. 13 months of listening history, alerts and Lumen listening.
  - **There is no free plan.**
  - **2026 changes:**
    - A fourth tier was added around late June or July 2026. Professional is now marked "most popular".
    - The top tier's list price rose from $249 to $399.
    - The entry tier went from $199 to $99. This was observed by CostBench and PricingSaaS from page snapshots; Hootsuite has not confirmed it.
    - The "skip trial" discount was removed.
    - Sources: costbench.com/changelog/hootsuite-plan-added-2026-07/; costbench.com/changelog/hootsuite-plans-renamed-2026-04/; pricingsaas.com/companies/hootsuite/diffs/2026W27.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **WhatsApp in the inbox:** WhatsApp Business has been supported since February 2024 (hootsuite.com/whats-new/whatsapp-integration), and WhatsApp Coexistence (Business app plus Cloud API) was added later. Which plans include WhatsApp is **unverified**; the update pages do not say.
  - **Smart Replies:** AI-suggested replies to comments, mentions and DMs, live since 2025-04-28 (hootsuite.com/whats-new/boost-inbox-efficiency-with-smart-replies).
  - **Per-profile brand voice (beta, 2025-06-03):** the AI learns tone from a profile's past posts (hootsuite.com/whats-new/personalize-owlygpt-responses-with-your-brand-voice-beta). There is no URL or document input.
  - **Listening depth:** Talkwalker (Lumen) gives 13 months of history at Enterprise, and benchmarking at every tier.
  - **Agent-ready data:** MCP connectors expose Hootsuite data to outside AI agents (2026-06-24 press release).
  - **No-card trial** with no automatic charge.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No guided brand-builder intake or site ingest with cited evidence (B1, B2, B3). Brand voice is inferred from past posts only.
  - No structured brand kit: purpose, positioning, audience, origin and messaging (B4). No kit versioning or export (B6).
  - Approvals only on Advanced ($399/user) (B5, R3). There is no record of approval reasons tied to brand facts.
  - No no-AI-slop pass and no claims-need-evidence gate on drafts (R2).
  - No Telegram in the inbox (R6). WhatsApp is present.
  - No on-chain or public verification of official posts or accounts (P1, P2, P3).
  - No AI persona registration or disclosure (R8).
  - No ambassador payouts or USDC (Y1, Y2, Y3). The advocacy product (Parliament) is employee-only and unpaid.
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Demo reports with sample data, so a new account never looks empty. JamJam could pre-render a sample Verify page and Ledger.
  - "No card, no automatic charge" trial wording, with plain daily caps instead of feature lockouts during the trial.
  - Publishing MCP connectors. JamJam's registry and Ledger would suit an MCP server.
  - Brand voice learned from existing posts, as a second input alongside the interview and site.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: Partly · Run: Yes · Prove: No · Pay: No

### Sprout Social (https://sproutsocial.com)
- **Category:** Premium social media management and social intelligence suite.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. On 2026-05-13 Sprout announced **Trellis**, an AI agent spanning Publishing, Listening, Smart Inbox and Reporting, with general availability in July 2026. **Trellis Studio** lets teams build custom AI workflows. A cheaper **Essentials** tier was added around 2026-04-10.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Sprout publishes and schedules posts, runs a Smart Inbox that combines comments and DMs across networks (including WhatsApp), and offers strong reporting. Listening and Premium Analytics are paid add-ons. Trellis adds a conversational analysis layer across the product.
- **Target customer:** Mid-market and enterprise social teams and agencies. Pricing is per seat from the first seat.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Start the trial with an email address. The trial is 30 days on the Professional plan, **no card required**.
  2. After signup you are prompted to connect a first social profile. An older Appcues teardown, date unstated, shows Twitter as a hard gate: the only way forward was to connect it.
  3. Answer a short question about your experience level and goals.
  4. Explore reporting dashboards loaded with demo data.
  5. Sprout's onboarding article recommends connecting profiles as early as possible so data starts flowing, then setting the account owner, plan level and billing contacts.
  - Inputs: social OAuth and a goals question. There is no URL ingest or brand interview. Time-to-first-output is not stated.
  - Sources: sproutsocial.com/trial/; support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051188072; goodux.appcues.com/blog/sprout-social-activation-focused-onboarding.
- **Pricing:** Per seat per month. Checked 2026-10-12 at sproutsocial.com/pricing.
  - **Essentials:** $79 annual or $99 monthly. 5 profiles, publishing and post-level reporting. No consolidated inbox, and add-ons cannot be bought on this tier.
  - **Standard:** $199 annual. 5 profiles, consolidated inbox, 10 brand keywords.
  - **Professional:** $299 annual. Unlimited profiles. Adds competitor, tag and paid insights, "Enhance Post by AI Assist" and approval workflows.
  - **Advanced:** $399 annual. Adds "Enhance Reply by AI Assist", inbox sentiment and team productivity and care reports.
  - **Enterprise:** custom.
  - All tiers include 100 AI agent credits per month.
  - Monthly prices are shown only for Essentials. Listening and Premium Analytics are add-ons from Standard up, with prices behind a demo request (unverified).
  - **2026 change:** Essentials was new around 2026-04-10, and the advertised starting price dropped from $199 to $79 (usagepricing.com, from Wayback snapshots). The other tiers have not changed since 2023 (NapoleonCat archive data, competitor-authored).
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **Multi-step approvals:** several steps, several approvers per step, and a choice of any or all approvers. Approvers can approve, reject with a note, edit or comment. Unapproved posts do not publish, and posts pending 90 days are auto-rejected. Approvals are on Professional and Advanced. Limits: no approval reports, no bulk approve, and no revoking an approval (support article 205974715).
  - **WhatsApp in the Smart Inbox:**
    - Supported: text and image messages, text templates, and inbox reporting.
    - Limits: you cannot start a chat with someone who has no history in the inbox; media and interactive templates are not supported; there is no chatbot.
    - Connecting it requires a Meta Business Manager admin and a fresh number.
    - Sources: support article 5829594503949.
  - **Reporting is the main reason people stay:** 38% of positive Reddit mentions cite it (NapoleonCat analysis of 226 comments, Aug 2026, competitor-authored). G2 rating is 4.4/5 from about 6.9k reviews.
  - **Longest no-card trial in the set** (30 days on a high tier).
  - **AI Assist** offers four tone styles on replies and post text (support article 20105027830669).
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, site ingest or coverage map (B1, B2, B3). Brand voice is not a feature; there are only four generic tones.
  - No brand kit artifact, versioning or export (B4, B6).
  - No slop pass or claims gate; AI Assist rewrites without checking facts (R2).
  - No Telegram (R6). The cheapest inbox costs $199 per seat.
  - Lead finding exists only through the paid Listening add-on (R7).
  - No provenance or verification layer (P1, P2, P3).
  - No AI persona disclosure (R8). No paid ambassador or USDC flows (Y1, Y2, Y3). Sprout's influencer and advocacy products are separate SKUs.
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Sprout's approval model of steps, approvers, any-or-all, and auto-expire. It is a mature reference for B5 and R3. JamJam's addition would be recording why each approval was given.
  - Its missing pieces (approval reporting, bulk approve, revoke) are visible gaps JamJam can fill. A Ledger view of approvals doubles as an approval report.
  - Demo-data dashboards on day one.
  - A low publishing-only entry tier that excludes the inbox. This is a known way to drop the headline price without giving away the costly part.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Yes · Prove: No · Pay: No

### Buffer (https://buffer.com)
- **Category:** SMB and creator social scheduler with a community inbox.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. Buffer last repriced in November 2025 per NapoleonCat. Older Pro, Premium and Business plans are legacy.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Buffer is simple scheduling across about 11 networks, with an AI Assistant in the composer, a "Community" inbox for replying to comments, and Insights analytics. It is priced per connected channel, with a real free plan.
- **Target customer:** Solo creators, small businesses and small teams.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Sign up for the Free plan, with no card. The sidebar then shows "1/3 channels connected".
  2. Connect a channel: Publish, then the profile avatar, then Channels, then grant permission, then "Add to Buffer".
  3. Set the time zone (it was auto-detected in one test) and posting times for each channel.
  4. Open "+ New", pick channels, then write or use the AI Assistant. One tester got a draft in about 15 seconds.
  5. Schedule with "Next available", "Prioritize", "Set date and time" or "Now".
  - Inputs: social OAuth only.
  - Trial: 14 days on Essentials or Team. If no payment details are added, the account reverts to Free. That implies no card is needed, but Buffer does not say so outright.
  - Sources: support.buffer.com/articles/getting-started-with-buffers-publishing-features-adhqleECyT; buffer.com/pricing; marcandrews.com/buffer-review/ (updated Aug to Oct 2026).
- **Pricing:** Per channel per month. Checked 2026-10-12 at buffer.com/pricing; the monthly figures are from NapoleonCat, Oct 2026.
  - **Free:** $0. Up to 3 channels, 10 queued posts per channel, 1 user, AI Assistant, community inbox, 5 AI reply suggestions per week, 30-day Insights.
  - **Essentials:** $5/channel/mo annual or $6 monthly. Unlimited posts (fair-use cap of 5,000 per channel), unlimited AI replies, full analytics. 1 user.
  - **Team:** $10/channel/mo annual or $12 monthly. Unlimited users, **approval workflows**, access levels and branded reports.
  - Volume discounts apply above 10 channels. Nonprofits get 50% off.
  - The page contradicts itself on the annual discount: it says both "save 2 months" and 20%.
  - **2025/26 change:** around January 2026 the Free plan gained comment replies, 1 saved reply and 5 AI reply suggestions per week. Reply support expanded to LinkedIn, X, Threads, Instagram, Facebook and Bluesky, and the "Engagement" inbox was renamed "Community" (pricingsaas.com 2025Q4 diff).
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **The most generous free tier in the segment:** no card, with the AI Assistant and inbox included (pricing page; marcandrews review).
  - **Broad community inbox:** Bluesky, Facebook Pages, Google Business, Instagram, LinkedIn pages and profiles, Mastodon, Threads, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and X.
  - **AI-generated disclosure toggle** in the composer (marcandrews review). This is a small but direct precedent for JamJam's disclosure features.
  - **Per-channel pricing** keeps small accounts cheap. Approvals arrive at $10 per channel instead of a $199+ seat.
  - **Public API on every plan**, including Free (1 key, 3,000 requests per month).
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, ingest or kit (B1, B2, B3, B4, B6). Voice is adjusted per post (casual or formal, shorter or longer) and not saved.
  - Approvals are on Team only and are basic (B5, R3).
  - No slop or claims gate. One reviewer found a false first-person claim and an em dash in an AI draft (R2), which is exactly the failure JamJam's gate targets.
  - No WhatsApp, Telegram or DMs. The inbox covers comments only (R6).
  - No listening or lead finder (R7).
  - No verification layer (P1, P2, P3).
  - No AI persona (R8). No ambassador payouts (Y1, Y2, Y3).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - A free tier that includes the AI and inbox, metered by weekly AI-reply quota rather than by locking features.
  - An AI-generated disclosure toggle in the composer, which maps to R8 and Y2.
  - Per-unit pricing (per channel or per brand) instead of per seat, which matches JamJam's "free seats for partners" model.
  - Show the channel count, as in "1/3 channels connected", as the progress indicator during onboarding.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Yes · Prove: No · Pay: No

### Later (https://later.com)
- **Category:** Visual-first social scheduler (Later Social) plus an influencer and creator marketing business.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):**
  - Live. The plan lineup was restructured on 2025-07-01 into Starter, Growth and Scale; Advanced and Enterprise self-serve tiers were retired.
  - Social Inbox replaced "Conversations". It is still **beta** and desktop-only (help article updated 2026-07-02).
  - X/Twitter was removed in 2025 (hackceleration review).
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Later is a drag-and-drop visual calendar with an Instagram grid preview, Link in Bio and analytics, built for Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest-heavy brands. Separate creator products (influencer platform, Mavely-powered affiliate tools, "Creator AEO") target brands paying creators.
- **Target customer:** Creators, social media managers at consumer and lifestyle brands, and enterprise brands for influencer programmes.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Create a Later account.
  2. Click "Start 14-Day Free Trial" in the desktop app, or go to Settings then Subscription.
  3. Choose a plan.
  4. At checkout, optionally add Social Sets or users, **enter card details** and choose yearly or monthly. The card is not charged during the trial, and billing starts automatically at the end unless you cancel.
  5. Connect social profiles through OAuth (8 platforms).
  6. Drag media into the visual calendar and preview the grid before publishing.
  - Inputs: social OAuth only.
  - **The card requirement is disputed.** Later's own help centre describes a card at checkout; a third-party review says no card is needed. The vendor source is treated as authoritative.
  - Sources: help.later.com/hc/articles/360060127733; help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059362253; hackceleration.com/labs/review/later.
- **Pricing:** Per account, bundled by "social sets". A set is one profile per network, up to 8 profiles. Checked 2026-10-12 at later.com/pricing; monthly figures from SocialPilot, checked 2026-09-25, competitor-authored.
  - **Starter:** $18.75/mo annual or $25 monthly. 1 set, 1 user, 30 posts per profile per month, 5 AI credits, 3 months of analytics. No inbox and no approvals.
  - **Growth:** $37.50 annual or $50 monthly. 2 sets, 2 users, 180 posts per profile, 50 AI credits, Social Inbox (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), **content approval and external comment workflows**, UGC collection, 1 year of analytics.
  - **Scale:** $82.50 annual or $110 monthly. 6 sets, 4 users, unlimited posts, 100 AI credits, competitive benchmarking, brand health and brand mentions, 2 years of analytics.
  - **Add-ons:** a set is $11.25. An extra user is $3.75 on the plan cards but $5 in the FAQ, so the page conflicts. 100 AI credits cost $3.75.
  - A limited Free plan is documented in the help centre but not on the pricing page.
  - Influencer programmes are quoted separately.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **Best-in-class visual planner** with an Instagram grid preview (hackceleration review).
  - **Link in Bio** with affiliate tracking. Later claims 1B+ tracked transactions (later.com).
  - **Approvals with external commenting** from the mid tier ($37.50), much cheaper than Hootsuite or Sprout.
  - **Strong creator-side business** (influencer platform, affiliate payouts via Mavely). This is the closest any suite here gets to JamJam's PAY leg.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, ingest, kit or voice profile; there is only an AI caption writer (B1, B2, B3, B4, B6, R1).
  - No slop or claims gate (R2).
  - No X publishing at all since 2025. LinkedIn is supported (R4).
  - Inbox covers Instagram and Facebook DMs plus Instagram, Facebook and TikTok comments only. There is no WhatsApp or Telegram, no AI reply, and it is still beta (R5, R6).
  - No lead finder (R7).
  - No verification layer (P1, P2, P3).
  - Influencer payouts are fiat or affiliate-based, enterprise-quoted, and run as a separate business. They are not curated, per-verified-piece USDC with stored compliance records (Y1, Y2, Y3).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Grid and feed preview before approval. Seeing the post in context helps the approver.
  - External commenting in approvals, so clients and partners comment without a paid seat. This maps to JamJam's free partner seats.
  - AI credits sold as a small, cheap add-on ($3.75 per 100) instead of forcing a tier upgrade.
  - Pitfall to avoid: Later's Trustpilot score is 1.3/5, dominated by renewal and refund disputes after trial conversion (per hackceleration). A card-at-trial model carries that risk.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Partly · Prove: No · Pay: Partly (fiat creator and affiliate programmes, separate product)

### Agorapulse (https://www.agorapulse.com)
- **Category:** Social media management suite for agencies and mid-market teams, known for its inbox and ROI reporting.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. **The free plan was discontinued on 2026-07-06** for new and existing users (Agorapulse help centre, quoted by SocialPilot). "Archie", a free AI post generator, remains as a separate free tool.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Agorapulse publishes to all major networks, runs a unified inbox with moderation rules, and offers ROI and competitor reporting. **Organization AI Context** is a brand-context layer that steers its AI writer and report summaries.
- **Target customer:** Agencies, multi-brand teams and mid-market companies.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Click "Try for free" and enter a work email and password, or use Google.
  2. Wizard page 1: name and phone number.
  3. Wizard page 2: sliders for how many profiles you manage and team size, plus drop-downs for current tool and referral source.
  4. Wizard page 3: organisation name, business category, team size and **website**. A social link or placeholder is allowed if there is no site.
  5. Click "Start my trial".
  6. Connect at least one social profile to finish setup.
  7. Onboarding emails follow.
  8. Optional: run the AI Context wizard (website, industry, brand type, competitors, tone, advanced instructions). It generates an editable context and shows a "Source used" view. **The AI Writing Assistant is not available during the trial.**
  - Trial: 30 days in two 15-day blocks; you extend once. **No card required.** Signup is desktop-only.
  - Sources: support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/8762489; support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/11748805; support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/12400727.
- **Pricing:** Per user per month. Checked 2026-10-12 at agorapulse.com/pricing.
  - **Standard:** $79 annual or $99 monthly. 10 profiles, basic reports and basic ROI. No approvals.
  - **Professional:** $119 or $149. Simple approvals and team reports.
  - **Advanced:** $149 or $199. Bulk approve, 5 shared calendars, advanced ROI, ads reporting, competitor benchmarking, 24 months of data.
  - **Custom:** quote. Unlimited profiles, **multi-step approvals**, **AI reply suggestions**, unlimited moderation rules, API.
  - Extra profiles cost $10-15 per month.
  - Advanced Listening is a paid add-on, demo only (price unverified).
  - X support is noted as an extra cost (TechRadar, updated 2026-02-28).
  - **2026 change:** the free plan was removed on 2026-07-06.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - **Wide inbox:** Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, Google Business, TikTok, Bluesky and **Reddit**. TechRadar calls the unified inbox a highlight. There is no WhatsApp or Telegram.
  - **Organization AI Context:** the closest thing in this segment to JamJam's brand kit. It takes a website plus industry, competitors and tone, generates an editable context, and shows the sources used.
    - The context overrides user prompts, so individual users cannot drift off-brand.
    - A layered setup supports agency, client and channel levels.
    - Source: support article 12400727.
  - **ROI reporting** ties social activity to web results (TechRadar).
  - **AI on every paid plan:** writing assistant, alt text and report summaries.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - The AI Context is free text. It has no section-by-section evidence or quotes, no Evidenced/Inferred/Missing coverage, and no interview (B1, B2, B3).
  - No kit structure (purpose, origin and the rest) and no versioning or export (B4, B6).
  - Approvals: none on Standard; multi-step only on Custom (B5, R3).
  - No slop or claims gate (R2).
  - AI reply suggestions are Custom only (R5). No WhatsApp or Telegram (R6).
  - Listening is an add-on (R7).
  - No verification layer (P1, P2, P3). No persona (R8). No pay (Y1, Y2, Y3).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - The "Source used" view on generated brand context. JamJam already goes further with quotes, so it should show them just as visibly.
  - Layered context (agency, then client, then channel tone) for multi-brand or agency use, which ties to JamJam's free partner seats.
  - Brand context that overrides user prompts. This is good design for brand safety, and JamJam's voice should be enforced the same way.

## Segment takeaways
- **Nobody in this segment builds a brand from evidence.** The closest are Agorapulse's Organization AI Context (website plus tone, editable, "Source used" view), Hootsuite's brand voice learned from past posts (beta), and Octolens's website-to-context step. None keeps quotes, shows coverage, or versions a kit. B1-B4 and B6 are open territory.
- **Approvals are a premium upsell everywhere.**
  - Hootsuite: Advanced, $399/user.
  - Sprout: Professional, $299/seat.
  - Agorapulse: multi-step on Custom only.
  - Buffer: Team, $10/channel.
  - Later: Growth, $37.50.
  - None of them gates on claims or AI slop, and none stores why something was approved. JamJam putting approval on every tier, with a record of reasons, is a real difference (B5, R2, R3).
- **AI reply assist is now standard but either gated or generic.**
  - Hootsuite Smart Replies: Professional and up.
  - Sprout: four tones, Advanced only.
  - Agorapulse: Custom only.
  - Buffer: free at 5 per week.
  - Later: none confirmed.
  - None drafts replies from a verified brand kit.
- **Unified inbox: WhatsApp exists only at the top end (Hootsuite, Sprout); Telegram is supported by none of the eight.** Telegram plus WhatsApp in one needs-reply view (R6) is unserved by the big suites. Only inbox-first tools such as Zernio, Unipile, Chatwoot and Trengo do it (Zernio list, Sept 2026, competitor-authored).
- **2026 pricing moves are downmarket.**
  - Sprout added a $79 Essentials tier with no inbox.
  - Hootsuite added a $99 entry tier, raised its top tier to $399, and cut the skip-trial discount.
  - Agorapulse killed its free plan on 2026-07-06.
  - Buffer made comment replies and AI suggestions free.
  - Listening consolidated: Hootsuite runs Talkwalker as "Lumen", and Zoom is buying Common Room.
- **Agents and MCP are the 2026 theme:** Hootsuite Wisdom and MCP connectors, Sprout Trellis and Trellis Studio, plus MCP servers at Common Room, Octolens and Syften. A JamJam MCP server for the registry and Ledger would be table stakes, not a differentiator. Verifiable provenance is still unique: no competitor touches P1-P3.
- **Onboarding norms to match:**
  - No-card trials: 14 days (Hootsuite, Buffer, Syften), 30 days (Sprout, Agorapulse), 7 days (Octolens). Later is the exception, asking for a card at checkout per its help centre.
  - Social OAuth as step one.
  - Demo-data dashboards (Sprout, Hootsuite).
  - The fastest first value comes from Octolens and Syften, which go from a URL to an auto-generated setup in about 2 minutes. JamJam's 15-minute intake needs to show value early, for example by displaying ingested facts within the first minute.
- **PAY is untouched.** Only Later runs paid creator programmes, as a separate, enterprise-quoted, fiat and affiliate business. Common Room finds superfans but cannot pay them. Curated ambassador campaigns with per-piece USDC and stored compliance records have no direct rival here.
