# Segment D: Web2 ambassador / creator / UGC payout platforms

Research date: 2026-10-12. Desk research only (vendor pages, help centres, Shopify App Store listings, G2/Capterra/Trustpilot, third-party pricing write-ups). No accounts created. Third-party pricing estimates are labelled as such; several come from competitor blogs (Creator Hero, Archive) and should be treated as indicative, not confirmed.

### Roster (https://www.getroster.com)
- **Category:** Ambassador + affiliate + creator/seeding program platform for Shopify DTC brands
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The pricing page metadata shows it was last modified 2026-10-08.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Roster recruits ambassadors from a brand's high-repeat Shopify buyers, recruitment forms and Instagram discovery, and runs them through programs, campaigns, "actions" (small point-earning tasks) and milestone tiers. It pulls in tagged Instagram/TikTok posts with usage-rights consent, lets the brand boost them as Meta partnership ads, and pays commissions and flat cash rewards via PayPal/Venmo with tax forms handled.
- **Target customer:** DTC/ecommerce brands on Shopify, BigCommerce, WooCommerce or a custom stack. The homepage says a good fit is a brand with about $1M+ revenue or ~10k customers, a photogenic product, and someone who can run the program a few hours a week.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  - Brand side, per the "How to set up a new program" help article:
    1. Book a demo. The FAQ describes it as a 30-minute walkthrough of program goals. Growth and above include a strategist.
    2. Create the program: name, colour, icon.
    3. Configure recruitment: an optional program-specific application form, manual or auto approval of applicants, and branded automated emails.
    4. Set up a personal discount and a shareable discount (Shopify).
    5. Set up the Welcome Campaign from a pre-filled template. It assigns the new member's first task.
    6. Set the referral commission (% or flat), with manual approval or auto-approval after a set period.
    7. Set the referral points that count toward milestones.
    8. Configure what the ambassador portal shows. The program is then ready to recruit.
  - Ambassador side: they apply through a branded application on the brand's own domain, then are approved manually or automatically. They land in the portal and get the Welcome Campaign as their first task.
  - Inputs asked for: a store connection, a discount structure and a commission rate. No brand interview, URL ingestion or document upload is documented.
  - Time to first output: not stated.
  - Trial: none documented. The Shopify listing has a "Start for free" button, but no Roster trial length is stated. Card requirement unverified.
  - Sources: support.getroster.com set-up article, getroster.com pricing FAQ.
- **Campaign / brief builder:** Campaigns start from a template or from scratch, with requirements such as an IG post, a TikTok post or a video upload. Each requirement is set to auto or manual approval, and manual approval can be bypassed for ambassadors carrying a whitelist tag. Rewards can be points, discount codes, product, cash or custom. Cash is paid only on completion, and cash amounts lock once the campaign is published (docs.getroster.com campaign FAQs).
- **Content approval:** Approval is set per requirement and reviewed in the campaign's Submissions tab. Actions have a "Needs Approval" toggle.
- **Disclosure/compliance:** No FTC disclosure feature is documented on the pricing page, homepage or docs pages checked. Fraud screening of ambassadors runs "before you pay them" (pricing page).
- **Payout rails and fees:** PayPal and Venmo bulk payouts on Starter. Automated monthly payouts on Growth and above. W-9, W-8BEN and 1099 handling. The Shopify listing also names ACH and multi-currency. Roster states "no commissions" and no transaction fees on referred revenue. Any PayPal/Venmo pass-through fees are unverified.
- **Verification of posted work:** Social listening auto-captures Instagram/TikTok posts that tag the brand, but no explicit verification step is documented. Submissions are verified by a human reviewer.
- **Pricing (checked 2026-10-12):**
  | Tier | Price | Limits |
  |---|---|---|
  | Starter | $599/mo flat, discount for annual billing | 1 program / 2 active campaigns / 10 active actions; PayPal + Venmo payouts; basic social listening |
  | Growth | Website: "Talk to sales". Shopify listing: $1,199/mo | 3 programs / 6 campaigns / 30 actions; 3 admins; automated payouts; strategist |
  | Pro | Website: "Talk to sales". Shopify listing: $2,299/mo | 6 programs / 15 campaigns / 60 actions; 6 admins; strategy check-ins |
  | Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited; managed program with a CSM |

  Billing basis is a flat subscription per plan, not per seat or per ambassador. Sources: getroster.com/pricing, apps.shopify.com/roster.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - Flat SaaS pricing with no take rate on referred sales. Roster markets this explicitly against percentage-fee affiliate tools (pricing page, Roster's own comparison blog dated 2026-09-18).
  - The Welcome Campaign template gives every new ambassador a first task straight away, so no one lands on an empty dashboard (set-up article).
  - Per-requirement auto/manual approval plus whitelist-by-tag cuts review load for trusted ambassadors (campaign FAQs).
  - UGC rights consent is captured in-platform, which feeds partnership ads (homepage).
  - Reviewers praise the support team and the simple PayPal/Venmo payouts: 4.8/5 from 21 reviews on Shopify, 4.5/5 from 64 on G2.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, evidence-cited facts, coverage map or brand kit (B1-B4). Brand voice lives only in campaign copy.
  - No approval gates with recorded who/when/why for brand identity (B5), and no kit versioning or export (B6).
  - No AI drafting in the brand voice, no anti-slop pass and no claims gate (R1, R2).
  - No FTC disclosure auto-insertion or compliance record (Y2).
  - No public verification or ledger of approved posts, and no on-chain record (P1-P3).
  - Payouts are fiat PayPal/Venmo/ACH. No USDC, and pay is not tied to a verified off-X piece (Y3).
  - No publishing or reply tooling of its own (R4, R5).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - The Welcome Campaign pattern: auto-assign an ambassador's first paid brief the moment they are approved.
  - Per-deliverable auto/manual approval, with whitelist-by-tag for trusted ambassadors (fits Y2 curated ambassadors).
  - Locking the payout amount once a campaign is published, which protects ambassadors and maps to the capped budget in Y1.
  - Running fraud screening before payout as a named step.
  - "No take rate" is the headline Roster competes on. JamJam's fee on USDC payouts needs a clear justification, for example provenance plus the compliance record.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Partly · Prove: No · Pay: Yes

### Club (formerly Brandbassador) (https://club.co)
- **Category:** Gamified brand-ambassador / community platform with a consumer ambassador app
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live, rebranded. brandbassador.com 302-redirects to club.co. Club's article "From Brandbassador to Club" says the platform was rebuilt from the ground up and repositioned as community infrastructure, with the same team. The article shows no rebrand date (unverified). The Shopify app developer is "Club AS", listed 2023-10-16.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Brands recruit ambassadors from customers, email lists and followers, then push "missions" from a library of 100+ templates (UGC, reviews, referrals, social posts) to a consumer app. Ambassadors earn points, cash, gift cards, product or commission. Automated workflows handle approvals and rewards.
- **Target customer:** Mid-market DTC/eCommerce brands with an engaged customer base. Creator Hero calls it a poor fit for small brands still testing ambassador marketing.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  - Brand side:
    1. Book a demo. A banner for Google visitors offers "your first 30 days free".
    2. Install the Shopify app (free listing) for discount codes, order tracking and segments.
    3. Recruit via recruitment tools, auto-recruit or a web embed.
    4. Build missions with Mission Maker or the 100+ templates.
    5. Segment ambassadors and set commission/discount levels.
    6. Ambassadors complete missions and the brand approves them.
    - A Shopify reviewer said onboarding explained each step.
    - Reported one-time onboarding fees are $1,000-$5,000 (third-party, Creator Hero).
  - Ambassador side, per the iOS listing:
    1. Download the Club app.
    2. Sign in with Apple, Google or TikTok. No follower minimum.
    3. Browse and join brands. Open missions are open to all; closed missions need a request (G2 discussion).
    4. Complete the mission (post, content, survey, share).
    5. The brand approves the post before payout, per app reviews.
    6. Earnings go to the Reward Wallet and can be converted to gift cards for a bonus.
  - Time to first output: not stated.
  - Card requirement: unverified.
  - Trial: 30 days free via a demo banner on the homepage. Creator Hero says there is no free trial and annual contracts are required, which conflicts with the banner. Treat the trial as a sales offer.
- **Campaign / brief builder:** Mission Maker plus a template library. Missions can target segments.
- **Content approval:** Approvals run through automated workflows (homepage FAQ). App reviews confirm brand approval gates payout.
- **Disclosure/compliance:** Not mentioned on the homepage, pricing page or app listing.
- **Payout rails and fees:** Automated cash, commission and gift-card payouts from an in-app wallet, with a bonus for taking gift cards. Payout rails and fees are not documented (unverified). Creator Hero reports a 5% commission on tracked sales for some sales features (third-party).
- **Verification of posted work:** App-side tracking of social posts, including like counts. One iOS review reports a like-counting glitch that recorded zero, which blocked payout, plus slow bug responses. Trustpilot's AI summary notes some reports of pending withdrawals and missing balances.
- **Pricing (checked 2026-10-12):**
  - Three editions, all "Request pricing" / "Contact us": Club Hub (Engage, Communicate, Affiliate), Club Suite (adds Insights, Manager) and Club Universe (adds Loyalty, Recruitment). Sources: club.co/pricing and G2.
  - The homepage FAQ says pricing scales with the number of active ambassadors and missions.
  - Third-party estimates (Creator Hero, 2026): about $2,000/mo for Growth and $3,000/mo for Pro, annual contracts, plus onboarding fees. These are unverified with the vendor.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - The ambassador-facing app is strong: 4.8/5 from 767 iOS ratings and a Trustpilot TrustScore of 4.8 from 891 reviews, mostly creators praising ease and fun.
  - Low-friction ambassador sign-up: social login and no follower minimum.
  - The 100+ mission templates mean brands don't start from a blank brief.
  - Open vs closed missions give a simple curated-vs-open switch.
  - The gift-card bonus nudges ambassadors toward cheaper payout rails.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand-building, evidence or kit features (B1-B6).
  - No AI voice drafting, slop pass or claims gate (R1, R2).
  - No disclosure auto-insertion or stored compliance record (Y2).
  - No public verification or ledger (P1-P3). Payout disputes rely on Club's internal records, and one reviewer advises ambassadors to screen-record their activity, which shows a trust gap that P3-style proof would close.
  - No USDC. Missions are mostly social posts, the opposite of JamJam's off-X stance (Y3).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - A mission template library for ambassador briefs (blog post, tutorial, docs PR, LinkedIn article).
  - Open vs closed (request-to-join) missions as the curation toggle.
  - Tiered payout options with a bonus for the cheaper rail. The JamJam equivalent would be a small USDC bonus vs fiat off-ramp.
  - Ambassadors' payout complaints show that a public, verifiable approval-and-payment record has value for the ambassador side too. Pitch it there, not only to brands.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Partly · Prove: No · Pay: Yes

### BrandChamp (https://www.brandchamp.io)
- **Category:** Ambassador / loyalty / referral program platform with a branded portal and app
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The pricing page and help centre are reachable.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** BrandChamp runs points-and-rewards ambassador programs ("BrandChamps") with activities, referral links, discount codes, commissions, a branded mobile app and portal, and a community feed. Ambassadors submit proof of activities, admins approve each task, and rewards are issued.
- **Target customer:** Shopify and WooCommerce ecommerce brands running loyalty-style or performance ambassador programs.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  - Brand side, per the Quick Start Guide:
    1. Set up commissions (% or fixed, with tiers).
    2. Create activity types: tasks, rewards, limits, availability and dates.
    3. Create reward types. Each can auto-issue or require admin review.
    4. Create referral link templates.
    5. Create discount code sets.
    6. Add members: approve applicants, add them manually or import a CSV.
    7. Optionally add yourself as a test user.
    8. Email ambassadors.
    - Time to launch: not stated. A Creator Hero summary says onboarding "can take real time to get right."
  - Ambassador side, per "Walk Through - Onboarding a BrandChamp":
    1. Fill in the application form at the store path, e.g. /community/ambassadors/apply.
    2. An admin accepts or rejects the application.
    3. The acceptance email carries a registration link. Only approved applicants can register.
    4. Complete the profile.
    5. Land in Activities, which is empty until the brand publishes activities.
    - No terms-acceptance step is documented.
  - Trial: none documented. Creator Hero says there is no free trial. Card requirement unverified.
- **Campaign / brief builder:** Activity types with multiple tasks, scheduling, segmentation (Essential+) and workflow automations (Pro+).
- **Content approval:** Per the walk-through on completing and approving an activity:
  1. The ambassador pastes a post link or picks a recent post via social auth.
  2. The admin sees an inline preview in "Awaiting Review".
  3. Each task is approved or rejected individually. A multi-task activity completes only when all required tasks are approved.
  4. A rejection carries a comment, and the ambassador can edit and resubmit.
  - "AI Automation (Soon)" on Premium is meant to moderate content and review applicants.
- **Disclosure/compliance:** None mentioned on the pricing page or the help pages checked.
- **Payout rails and fees:**
  - Points, a catalog, discount codes, gift cards and product via Shopify/WooCommerce. Cash activity rewards from Essential.
  - The pricing page says "No hidden transaction, storage, or payment processing fees."
  - The cash payout rail is not documented (unverified).
- **Verification of posted work:** The post picker uses social auth to select the ambassador's own real posts, and an inline preview supports human review. No automated check.
- **Pricing (checked 2026-10-12):**
  - The vendor site lists tiers but no prices ("Book a Demo" / "Get a Custom Quote").
  - The Shopify App Store lists prices in USD, billed every 30 days, with annual billing saving 17%:
    | Tier | Monthly | Annual | Ambassadors included | Overage per extra ambassador/mo |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Launch | $520 | $5,200 | 350 | $0.75 |
    | Essential | $850 | $8,500 | 1,000 | $0.50 |
    | Pro | $1,500 | $15,000 | 3,000 | $0.35 |
    | Premium | $2,750 | $27,500 | 12,000 | $0.20 |
    | Enterprise | Custom (vendor site) | | | |
  - Billing basis: per plan, plus per-ambassador overage. Admins, missions and segments are unlimited.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - A clear per-task approval model with rejection comments and a resubmit loop (help article).
  - The social-auth post picker reduces fake or mistyped proof links.
  - Transparent per-ambassador overage pricing on Shopify, with no processing fees claimed.
  - A branded app and portal on every tier (Creator Hero, 2026-07-10).
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, evidence, coverage map or kit (B1-B6).
  - No AI drafting, voice templates, slop pass or claims gate (R1, R2).
  - No disclosure insertion or compliance record (Y2).
  - No public proof (P1-P3).
  - No stablecoin rail (Y3).
  - Ambassadors land on an empty Activities screen after registering, which is a weak first-run experience.
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Per-task approval with mandatory rejection reasons and resubmission. Store the reasons in JamJam's compliance record (Y2).
  - The "Pick from your posts" pattern: authenticate to the source and choose the real artifact instead of pasting a URL. For off-X pieces, this could be blog RSS or GitHub PR pickers.
  - Per-ambassador overage pricing is a clean model for "ambassadors are free seats up to N".
  - The "add yourself as a test ambassador" step in brand onboarding.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Partly · Prove: No · Pay: Partly (points and catalog are strong, the cash rail is undocumented)

### SocialLadder (https://socialladderapp.com)
- **Category:** Mobile-first ambassador management (campus, field, event and online programs)
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The blog was updated July 2026. The /pricing URL returned a fetch error, so pricing details are from the homepage and third parties.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** SocialLadder identifies, tracks, rewards and engages ambassadors through gamified "challenges", including offline activity such as events and sampling. It added AI content feedback in 2026, which pre-screens submissions against brand-specific criteria.
- **Target customer:** Brands with large ambassador communities, including campus and field programs (Roster's comparison blog). Mid-market to enterprise.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  - Brand side:
    1. Book a demo for a custom quote.
    2. A setup fee and contract follow.
    3. Onboarding takes about 10-14 days and includes technical training, using customizable templates for the application, handbook and terms (homepage).
    4. Launch challenges.
    - The step-level walkthrough is not public (unverified).
  - Ambassador side: apply via the template application, accept the handbook and terms, then use the mobile app. Details beyond the templates are unverified.
  - Trial: none (Influencer Hero 2026). Card requirement: not applicable, sales-led.
- **Campaign / brief builder:** Challenges, including conversion and milestone challenges and a Complete Action challenge that tracks actions in the brand's own app or site without screenshots (blog, 2024-02-05).
- **Content approval:** AI content feedback reviews submissions against brand-specific criteria before final human review (blog, 2026-05-06). The vendor claims "90% accuracy" and some clients saving 80+ hours a week.
- **Disclosure/compliance:** Not documented. The AI criteria are brand-defined and may include hashtags, but whether they check disclosure is unverified.
- **Payout rails and fees:** Rewards are mentioned in general. Rails and fees are undocumented. A Capterra reviewer reports recurring payout issues.
- **Verification of posted work:** Post tracking exists, but reviewers report missing posts in reports and inaccurate engagement rates (Capterra UK, 4.5/5 from 6 reviews).
- **Pricing (checked 2026-10-12):**
  - Vendor: no prices. Cost is a setup fee plus a recurring base subscription plus seat fees where applicable (homepage). Capterra calls it usage-based.
  - Third-party (Influencer Hero, updated 2026-08-19): Starter/Mid-Market from just under $2,000/mo with a roughly six-month minimum. Enterprise and the API add-on are custom and annual. All unverified with the vendor.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - AI pre-review gives the ambassador feedback before human review, a draft-improve loop on the ambassador side.
  - Offline and event activity tracking, which most competitors lack.
  - Screenshot-free action verification for in-app or on-site actions.
  - Reviewers find the admin and ambassador interface intuitive, and challenge creation and approvals easy (Capterra UK).
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand kit or evidence (B1-B6).
  - The AI review is not tied to an evidenced brand voice or claims gate (R2).
  - No disclosure auto-insertion (Y2).
  - No public proof (P1-P3). Its reporting reliability problems are exactly what verifiable records address.
  - No USDC (Y3).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Run JamJam's slop pass, claims gate and voice-fit on ambassador submissions as pre-review feedback to the ambassador, not only as a brand-side gate.
  - Ship onboarding templates (application, handbook, terms) that the brand edits. JamJam can generate these from the brand kit.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Partly · Prove: No · Pay: Partly

### CreatorIQ (https://www.creatoriq.com)
- **Category:** Enterprise creator marketing suite (discovery, workflow, payments, measurement, brand safety)
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The "new CreatorIQ" platform and SafeIQ launched 2025-10-21. CreatorIQ Pay expanded on 2025-12-04.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** CreatorIQ is an end-to-end enterprise platform. It covers creator discovery and evaluation, campaign execution with briefs and bulk content approval, contracts and payments (CreatorIQ Pay), measurement and benchmarking (BenchmarkIQ). SafeIQ is a multimodal AI brand-safety product that reviews creators' content before activation and monitors live partners.
- **Target customer:** Global enterprises, agencies and large DTC brands. The site says 1,300+ brands and agencies.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  - Brand side:
    1. Request pricing / demo.
    2. Annual contract.
    3. Implementation, which may include professional services fees (Archive, 2026-04-29). G2 users report about 2 months to implement.
    4. Strategic Services offers workshops and governance frameworks (2025-10-21 release).
  - Campaign setup is a step-by-step flow: invite creators, share briefs, approve content in bulk (campaign management page).
  - Creator side: centralized creator onboarding with automated contracts. Pay collects tax and banking details automatically.
  - Trial: none. Card: not applicable, sales-led.
- **Campaign / brief builder:** A step-by-step campaign requirements flow with brief sharing.
- **Content approval:** Centralized review and approval for organic and paid content, shown next to performance. Bulk approval is available.
- **Disclosure/compliance:**
  - The platform claims "always-on compliance and NSFW safeguards" (2025-10-21 release).
  - SafeIQ does multimodal brand-safety checks before activation ("Lookback") and ongoing monitoring.
  - No page checked explicitly documents FTC disclosure detection (unverified).
- **Payout rails and fees:**
  - CreatorIQ Pay: fund accounts in 22 currencies and pay out in 60+ currencies across 80+ markets, using local-to-local transfers to cut FX costs.
  - US tax forms are automated.
  - Campaign managers approve payments, and funds release once the creator's payment details are on file.
  - Live for US/Canada entities. UK/EU and APAC are listed as coming soon.
  - Fees are not published (unverified).
- **Verification of posted work:** AI collects brand-mentioning posts within the campaign window, with keyword refinement. Metrics refresh every 8 hours.
- **Pricing (checked 2026-10-12):**
  - Vendor: quote only (pricing request page).
  - Third-party (Archive, citing Vendr data, 2026-04-29): about $30k/yr entry, $39,250 median, $59,500 high, around $50k/yr for enterprise packages. Annual only. Unverified with the vendor.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - Global payouts with approval-before-release and a finance audit trail (CreatorIQ Pay release).
  - Bulk content approval next to performance data.
  - SafeIQ learns brand-specific severity thresholds instead of using static keyword filters (2025-10-21 release).
  - Scale and reputation: 4.6/5 from 583 reviews on G2.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, evidence-cited facts or brand kit (B1-B4).
  - No anti-slop or claims gate on creator drafts. SafeIQ covers brand safety, not claim evidence (R2).
  - FTC disclosure insertion is not documented (Y2).
  - The audit trail is private to the brand. No public Verify or Ledger (P3).
  - No stablecoin rail (Y3).
  - Price and implementation time put it out of reach of JamJam's first buyer (crypto/dev brands).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Treat "approve deliverable, then release funds" as a single action with an audit trail. JamJam's version should write the approval record and trigger the USDC payout in one step.
  - Show performance next to the approval queue.
  - SafeIQ's adaptive severity is worth borrowing for JamJam's claims gate: learn from what the brand overrides.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Partly · Prove: No · Pay: Yes

### GRIN (https://grin.co)
- **Category:** Creator management platform (CRM, gifting, affiliate, payments) with an AI agent, "Gia"
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The pricing model has changed in 2026, from quote-only annual contracts to self-serve month-to-month tiers with a free plan. Third-party pages show different tier sets within 2026, which suggests the tiers changed more than once.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** GRIN is a creator CRM with discovery, outreach, gifting, affiliate links and codes, content library, payments and reporting, built around deep Shopify integration. Its AI, Gia, prepares work daily (briefing, shortlists, campaigns) and the team approves "the moments that matter."
- **Target customer:** Ecommerce/DTC brands with a dedicated creator program owner (Creator Hero, 2026-08-04).

## Segment takeaways
- **Nobody builds the brand.** All seven core platforms assume the brand already knows its voice and claims. Briefs and guidelines are typed by hand (Aspire's guideline checklist is the most structured). JamJam's B1-B4 has no equivalent here, and generating the brief's guideline checklist from an evidenced kit is a clear wedge.
- **Content approval before payout is table stakes.** Roster, BrandChamp, Aspire, CreatorIQ, Club and SideShift all gate rewards on a human approval. The differentiators are per-guideline change requests (Aspire), per-task rejection with resubmit (BrandChamp), auto-approve for trusted ambassadors (Roster) and AI pre-review (SocialLadder). JamJam's approval is not novel. What is novel is making the approval record portable and public.
- **FTC disclosure is the weakest area across the segment.**
  - No core competitor documents auto-inserted disclosure.
  - Aspire leaves it to the brand's own T&Cs PDF and a hashtag checklist.
  - impact.com and Valen Sentinel detect or score disclosure, after posting or as a pre-check.
  - Y2 (auto-insert, plus approval stored as a compliance record) is a real gap. Pitch it as "disclosure you can't forget, with receipts".
- **Payout rails are PayPal/Venmo/ACH, plus CreatorIQ's 60+ currency local-to-local rails.**
  - Fee models vary: Aspire charges 2% handling plus processing, while Roster, BrandChamp and GRIN claim no take rate.
  - Ambassador-side complaints cluster on payouts: Club (pending withdrawals, a like-count glitch blocking payout), SocialLadder (payout issues on Capterra).
  - Fast, final USDC with a public receipt answers a documented pain. JamJam's fee on payouts will be compared against "no take rate" SaaS.
- **Verification of posted work is weak.** Most platforms rely on social listening (Roster, CreatorIQ) or a pasted or picked link plus human review (BrandChamp, Aspire). Reviewers report missed posts and bad counts (SocialLadder, Club). A public Verify page showing official, edited or not found (P3) has no peer in this segment.
- **Pricing bands:**
  - Self-serve GRIN: $0-$1,000/mo, month-to-month, credit-metered, free tier with no card.
  - Flat ambassador SaaS: Roster from $599/mo, BrandChamp $520-$2,750/mo plus per-ambassador overage.
  - Sales-led mid-market: Club, SocialLadder and Aspire at about $2k/mo with annual minimums (third-party).
  - Enterprise: CreatorIQ at about $30-60k/yr (third-party).
  - JamJam's TBD subscription needs to sit at or under the GRIN and Roster entry points to win crypto/dev brands.
- **Everyone is social-first** (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube). None targets paid off-social technical pieces (blogs, tutorials, docs, LinkedIn articles) the way Y3 does. This is a positioning gap for dev/crypto brands, and also a sign the off-X market is unproven.
- **Patterns to adopt:**
  - Roster's Welcome Campaign (first brief auto-assigned on approval).
  - Club's mission template library and open vs closed missions.
  - Aspire's brief schema with a T&Cs acknowledgment.
  - GRIN's "agent prepares, human approves" desk.
  - Valen's creator self-audit before submission.
