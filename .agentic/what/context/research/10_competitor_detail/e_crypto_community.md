# Segment E: Crypto-native community and creator-pay platforms

Research date: 2026-10-12. Desk research only; no accounts created. Prices are in USD as listed. "Unverified" means no public source confirmed the fact; the reason is given each time.

## Context: X's January 2026 policy on paying for posts

- On Jan 15, 2026, X product lead Nikita Bier said X had changed its developer API policy. Apps that reward users for posting are no longer allowed, and X had already revoked API access from several of them. He blamed these apps for a large volume of AI slop and reply spam. (The Block, https://www.theblock.co/post/385805/kaito-token-plummets-x-revises-api-policies-ban-infofi-crypto-projects)
- Per The Block's follow-up, the rule covers tokens, points or cash given for engagement. X said it still supports other crypto and Web3 uses of the API. (https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-01-16-x-crackdown-ai-reply-spam-infofi-seek-alternatives-criminally-unimaginative-385847)
- Reported API revocations included Kaito, Wallchain and Noise (Today in DeFi, https://news.todayindefi.com/p/x-bans-infofi-30-vested-rewards-apr). Noise told The Block it was unaffected.
- What happened next:
  - Kaito shut down Yaps and moved to Kaito Studio.
  - Cookie DAO shut down Snaps.
  - Xeet paused all campaigns, then relaunched as "verified results from any platform".
  - Wallchain moved to Wallchain Select, which matches creators to brands with fixed terms.
- **Open risk for quest platforms:** Galxe (the "X Bullish About" credential, a tweet task) and Zealy (Tweet tasks, plus a "Top" reward method that ranks tweets by performance) still document paid X-post tasks as of Aug–Sep 2026. I found no public statement from X or from either platform about whether these tasks comply.
- **Why this matters for JamJam:** Y3 (no paying for X posts; pay only for off-X work) is a direct, defensible answer to the policy. Kaito's Katalyst says it now runs under a data agreement with X, so the incumbents are negotiating access rather than avoiding X.

### Kaito Studio (https://studio.kaito.ai/brands)
- **Category:** Curated creator marketplace and creator-campaign operator (successor to the Yaps leaderboards).
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. Yaps was sunset on Jan 15, 2026, and the Studio beta launched in March 2026 with 16 brand partners. Katalyst, a pay-for-results reward layer, launched on Jul 29, 2026. The brands page now lists three modes: Katalyst (open contest), Select (Kaito matches creators, the brand picks the final list) and Partner (pay for results).
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Brands launch creator campaigns on X, TikTok and YouTube. Kaito matches creators using its influence data, which it says comes from licensed access to X's own data. Kaito tracks clicks, sign-ups and mindshare back to each creator and pays every creator on the brand's behalf.
- **Target customer:** Crypto, finance and AI brands ("frontier tech"). The beta campaigns came from crypto projects such as StandX, Theoriq and Flipster.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. The brand clicks "Work with Kaito" on studio.kaito.ai/brands.
  2. It fills in a form: company email, name, company/product, website, socials (optional), Telegram (optional), campaign goal, budget range, timeline and up to 300 characters of extra detail.
  3. Kaito follows up. The rest is sales-led: Kaito matches creators, and in Select mode the brand picks the final list.
  4. The brand posts campaign info: objectives, scope, timeline, reward structure and brand guidelines.
  5. Approved creators submit reward quotes, and the brand selects the creators it wants. Each selected creator signs a contract directly with the brand.
  6. The creator submits the content URL, and Kaito reviews it.
  7. Kaito settles payment.
  - There is no self-serve trial and no published time to first output. Card required: not applicable (contact sales).
  - Sources: the Work with Kaito form (https://studio.kaito.ai/dashboard/work-with-kaito) and the Studio Terms (https://docs.kaito.ai/legal/terms-of-use-studio).
- **Pricing:**
  - Studio brand pricing: unverified. Budget is only a range field on the request form.
  - Platform fee: the Studio Terms let Kaito take a fee on each creator's rewards, deducted at settlement. The amount is not published (terms page).
  - Katalyst (TGE campaigns): no service fee. The team posts a refundable deposit with the reward pool, and each token pool splits 80% to creators with verified conversions and 20% to KAITO stakers and YT-sKAITO holders (mpost.io, Jul 29, 2026).
  - Creator payouts are in digital assets to a self-custodial wallet (CoinGecko, Apr 2026).
  - Beta ambassador listings quoted fixed fees of $1,000 to $1,500 a month plus a bonus (AiCoin, Mar 6, 2026).
  - Checked 2026-10-12.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - Kaito says it is the only creator platform with licensed access to X data, and Katalyst adds a data-cooperation agreement with X plus Brevis zero-knowledge verification. Of everyone in this segment, it is best placed with X.
  - Attribution goes beyond impressions to clicks, sign-ups, deposits and in-platform activity, through tracking and a browser extension (Kaito Pulse).
  - Kaito handles payouts end to end and pays every creator on the brand's behalf.
  - Published case metrics include CPM: a $5 CPM TikTok/YouTube video contest for Axie Infinity and an $8 CPM open campaign (brands page).
  - Scale: Kaito claims $115M+ in creator rewards paid out, 200K+ active creators and 20M+ reachable creators (brands page; self-reported).
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand-building intake, evidence-cited facts, coverage map or brand kit (B1, B2, B3, B4).
  - No approval gates recorded as compliance artifacts, and no versioned brand kit (B5, B6).
  - No brand-voice drafting or AI-slop/claims gate; creators write their own content (R1, R2).
  - No owned-channel publishing, reply queue or inbox (R4, R5, R6).
  - No onchain registration of brand kit, posts or claims, and no public Verify page (P1, P2, P3). Kaito's "verification" checks conversions, not authenticity.
  - Creators must use a self-custodial wallet (P4 contrast).
  - Still pays for X posts, now under an X data deal, so it is the opposite of Y3. I found no public mention of auto-inserted FTC disclosure (Y2).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Three campaign modes on one marketplace (open contest, curated shortlist, pay for results) make an easy menu for Y1.
  - Publish CPM and cost-per-result figures on case studies.
  - The creator reward-quote flow (creator bids within the brand's range, brand accepts) is a good pattern for Y1 per-piece pricing.
  - A refundable brand deposit for each campaign signals commitment to creators.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: Partly · Prove: No · Pay: Yes

### Galxe Quest with Business+ (https://dashboard.galxe.com/business)
- **Category:** Onchain quest, loyalty-points and credential platform with paid business tiers.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. The business plan is branded "Business+" with tiers Basic, Essentials, Growth and Enterprise. ("Business" with no plus is not a separate current tier.) Help articles were updated Aug–Sep 2026.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Projects create a Space and run no-code quests. Each quest combines social, onchain and custom-API tasks ("credentials") and rewards completers with points, OATs (onchain achievement tokens), NFTs, tokens or Discord roles. Paid tiers add bot and Sybil detection, analytics, verified X actions and promotion on Galxe's own surfaces.
- **Target customer:** Web3 projects from early-stage to large ecosystems. Galxe claims 7,800+ projects (sponsored article in The Block, Feb 2026).
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Sign in to Galxe with an EVM wallet such as MetaMask.
  2. Go to dashboard.galxe.com/onboard.
  3. Choose a Business+ plan. The free Basic plan requires a 300 USDT/USDC deposit, accepted on ETH, BSC, Polygon or Solana.
  4. Confirm the account, fill in a survey and accept the terms.
  5. Enter project details: name, intro, logo, category and token info.
  6. Bind an official space email.
  7. Pay the deposit or subscription.
  8. The Space is created. Next, build a quest from templates: choose tasks and credentials, then rewards. Token rewards need a deposit of at least $10 to publish.
  - No time to first output is stated. No trial; the free path is Basic with the deposit. Card: no; payment is in stablecoins from a wallet or Gas Station funds.
  - Source: https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/8506982-getting-started-with-galxe-quest
- **Pricing (Business+ Overview, updated Aug 10, 2026; checked 2026-10-12):**
  - Basic: $0 a month, with a 300 USDT/USDC deposit.
  - Essentials: $1,166 a month paid quarterly, or $999 a month paid annually ($11,988 a year).
  - Growth: $1,999 a month paid quarterly, or $1,699 a month paid annually ($20,388 a year). Adds social and in-app promotion, plus guaranteed co-marketing each quarter (homepage carousel, a dedicated X post, and more). Includes a $300 monthly gas credit in the help article's example.
  - Enterprise: custom.
  - Add-ons: Space verification is $350 a year (included while subscribed). The OAT claim fee is $0.08 on some chains unless the quest is gasless. X Authentic Verification uses credits and needs Rewards Hub Level 2+ or Business+.
  - Billing is per Space; no per-seat or usage pricing is listed beyond credits.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - Bot identification sorts participants into real, suspicious and bot, and can auto-exclude bots from rewards (Business+ Overview).
  - Verification is broad: onchain credentials, NFT-holder gates, custom API tasks, and X Authentic Verification that confirms a completed action rather than a page visit (Tasks Settings article).
  - Reward rails are flexible and onchain: points contracts deployable on Gravity, Solana, Sui or Sei, plus OATs, mystery boxes and token raffles recorded onchain (Rewards and Points articles).
  - Distribution: subscribers get guaranteed promotion slots across Galxe's surfaces each quarter. Galxe cites about 184M quest participations in 2025 (Messari figure via a sponsored article in The Block).
  - API access lets brands show quest completions and leaderboards on their own site.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, evidence-cited kit, coverage map or voice (B1–B4, R1).
  - No AI-slop or claims gate, and no human approval before posts go out. Participants post their own content, and tasks verify the action, not message quality (R2, R3).
  - No owned-channel publishing, reply queue or inbox (R4–R6).
  - Credentials prove that a task was done, not that content is official. No brand-kit or post registry and no Verify page (P1–P3).
  - Wallet login is required for the brand (P4).
  - Open quests rather than curated ambassadors, and no FTC disclosure tooling (Y2).
  - The "X Bullish About" credential pays for positive tweets, which conflicts with Y3 and carries X-policy risk.
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Quarterly-vs-annual pricing with an explicit annual total.
  - A free tier secured by a refundable deposit instead of a card. This fits crypto brands, and could map to a USDC deposit that funds the first ambassador campaign.
  - Guaranteed promotion bundled into the mid tier.
  - Real/suspicious/bot labels on participant reports.
  - The help centre's "on-chain vs off-chain rewards" table is clear about which actions leave a public record. JamJam's Ledger could explain itself the same way.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: No · Prove: Partly (onchain credentials) · Pay: Yes (tokens, not curated)
- **Sources:** https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/11568587-business-overview ; https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/8506982-getting-started-with-galxe-quest ; https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/9645630-create-quest-rewards ; https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/9728885-create-quest-tasks-settings ; https://help.galxe.com/en/articles/9733873-advanced-points-management ; https://www.theblock.co/post/388952/how-galxe-business-helps-teams-scale-at-every-stage

### Zealy (https://zealy.io)
- **Category:** Community quest and leaderboard platform (formerly Crew3).
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. A new Free-plan limit of 100 members takes effect Oct 1, 2026.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Communities build quests of up to five tasks each. Tasks verify automatically where possible (X, Discord, API) or go to manual or AI review. Members earn XP, leaderboard rank, sprint prizes and rewards; USDC is paid automatically and guaranteed by Zealy.
- **Target customer:** Crypto communities and project teams running always-on community programs. The docs stress long-term communities over one-off campaigns.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Create a community at zealy.io/create-community.
  2. Set the name, URL, blockchain, security settings and member permissions.
  3. Create a module, then quests from a template, a new page or inline.
  4. Add tasks; most verify automatically, and manual checks go to the review panel.
  5. Add rewards (XP, Discord role, Zaps, USDC, token, custom) and a reward method: All, first come first served, Raffle, Top or Vote.
  6. Publish, or save as a draft.
  7. Optionally plan a sprint, a time-limited leaderboard with a USDC reward pool.
  - Free plan with no card. Paid trial: none described.
  - Sources: https://zealy.io/docs/start-guide/community-setup ; https://zealy.io/docs/quests/create-quests
- **Pricing (pricing page and Billing docs; checked 2026-10-12):**
  - Free: $0, with no card. 1,000 claims a month and up to 100 members (from Oct 1, 2026). X quest tasks and analytics are not included.
  - Standard: $149 a month billed annually, or $199 a month for 6 months upfront. Unlimited members, 10,000 claims a month, extra credits at $15 per 1,000. Adds X tasks, analytics, Proof of Humanity and a listing on Zealy.
  - Plus: $359 a month billed annually, $439 a month for 6 months, or $549 a month billed monthly. 50,000 claims a month, extra credits at $12 per 1,000. Adds CRM, AI review, X quest automation and 4 homepage promotion days a month.
  - Enterprise and agencies: custom, billed annually. Unlimited usage, an account manager, a Telegram and X booster package, and the option to pay in crypto.
  - Usage metering: each claim costs 1 credit. X tasks add credits: tweet +1, retweet +2, like +3, X Premium check +2. Credit top-ups go through Stripe. Prices exclude VAT.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - Usage pricing is transparent: the quest editor shows the live credit cost per claim before you publish (Billing docs).
  - USDC rewards are locked from the community balance when the quest is created, so payout is guaranteed. Tiered sprint payouts give everyone in a rank band the same amount (Rewards and Sprint docs).
  - AI review with a custom prompt, plus engagement thresholds (minimum likes, impressions) on tweet tasks.
  - API tasks let the brand verify actions in its own product, such as a newsletter sign-up or purchase (changelog).
  - The free tier is a working plan, not a trial: no time limit and no card.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand building, kit or voice (B1–B6, R1).
  - No claims gate or slop pass on member content; AI review checks quest compliance, not brand truth (R2).
  - No owned publishing, reply queue or inbox (R4–R6).
  - No onchain proof or Verify page (P1–P3).
  - Rewards are open to any member rather than curated per piece (Y2).
  - Paid X tasks, including tweet performance rewards, conflict with Y3 and carry X-policy risk.
  - Crypto payment is Enterprise-only, and no fee on payouts is disclosed.
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Lock the USDC budget at campaign creation. This maps directly to Y1's capped budget.
  - Show the live cost per approved piece before launch.
  - Tiered payout bands.
  - A free plan that is useful on its own; drive upgrades with usage caps, not time limits.
  - Offer 6-month billing as a middle option between monthly and annual.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: No · Prove: No · Pay: Yes
- **Sources:** https://zealy.io/pricing ; https://zealy.io/docs/start-guide/billing-and-plans ; https://zealy.io/docs/start-guide/community-setup ; https://zealy.io/docs/quests/create-quests ; https://zealy.io/docs/quest-properties/rewards ; https://zealy.io/docs/leaderboard/create-sprint ; https://zealy.io/docs/tasks/twitter ; https://www.zealy.io/changelog

### Layer3 (https://layer3.xyz)
- **Category:** Onchain activation (quest) marketplace with CUBE credentials; adding Competitions and Perks.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. Perks launched in March 2026, and trading Competitions were live in August 2026 (outposts.io; CoinMarketCap updates, Oct 2026).
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Protocols create "Activations": onchain tasks that users complete and record by minting a CUBE credential. Protocol-funded rewards go out in tokens, points, NFT allowlists or raffles. Layer3 curates which Activations go live and markets them to its user base.
- **Target customer:** Onchain protocols, L1/L2 ecosystems and dApps that want verified onchain usage.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Go to app.layer3.xyz/builder and click "Create Your Space".
  2. Fill in the required details.
  3. Enable two-factor authentication.
  4. Stake L3 on Ethereum mainnet for at least 12 months to unlock the Space.
  5. Create an Activation: title, description, image, difficulty and tags, schedule.
  6. Choose the audience.
  7. Set rewards and the CUBE chain. An incentive is mandatory, and rewards must be on the same chain as the CUBE.
  8. Preview, then submit for review. Approved Activations go live in 24–48 hours.
  - Layer3 may refuse Activations with no incentive, poor UX or unaudited dApps. No trial. Card: no (token stake).
  - Sources: https://layer3xyz.gitbook.io/layer3-updated-docs/builder/getting-started/getting-started--getting-access ; https://layer3xyz.gitbook.io/layer3-updated-docs/builder/guides/guides--creating-your-first-activation
- **Pricing:**
  - Self-serve Builder: an L3 stake on Ethereum for 12+ months. The amount required is unverified; the docs I read do not state it.
  - Managed contracts: mid five figures (seed apps) to low seven figures (L1/L2 foundations), per Alea Research (Jun 2025, third-party, may be outdated).
  - CUBE mint fees are paid by users in ETH, USDC, L3 or SOL and split between the Activation creator and the protocol. Creators earn 0% until they refer 50 new users (Creator Rewards docs).
  - Checked 2026-10-12.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - Verification is onchain by design: completion is proven by a minted credential, not by social data, so X policy does not affect it.
  - Curated review screens out low-quality or unaudited campaigns before launch.
  - CUBE holder lists act as an onchain CRM for retargeting and allowlists (CUBEs docs).
  - Scale: $35M+ in token rewards paid and 1,000+ protocols (FAQ; self-reported).
  - Brands earn revenue from Activations (a fee share), which offsets cost.
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand building, kit or voice (B1–B6, R1).
  - No content workflow at all (R2–R9).
  - Proves user actions, not content authenticity, and has no Verify page for brand messages (P2, P3).
  - Brands need wallets and must stake a token (P4).
  - Pays users for onchain actions, not ambassadors per verified piece of content (Y1–Y3).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Curated review with a stated go-live time (24–48 hours) and published refusal criteria.
  - Credential-as-receipt: each approved ambassador piece could produce an attestation the ambassador can show as portable proof of work.
  - Fee share back to the brand.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: No · Prove: Partly (user-action credentials) · Pay: Partly (user rewards, not creator pay)

### Superteam Earn, sponsor side (https://superteam.fun/earn/sponsor/)
- **Category:** Solana-native bounty, freelance and grant marketplace. Open source (SuperteamDAO/earn).
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. A "Pro" premium-listings section and an Agent API (agent submissions claimed by a human) also exist.
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** Solana projects post Bounties (open contests; the best entries win) or Projects (applications; the sponsor picks a freelancer). Talent submits work, and the sponsor reviews entries and announces winners. Winners are paid in crypto, typically USDC on Solana, to the wallet on their Earn profile.
- **Target customer:** Solana ecosystem teams (Jupiter, Helius, Solana Foundation and Civic are quoted). Any Solana project can sponsor.
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Create a sponsor profile at superteam.fun/earn/new/sponsor.
  2. Write a brief with rewards. The site says posting takes under 2 minutes.
  3. Publish a Bounty or Project.
  4. Review submissions. If the sponsor is short on time, a Superteam "brain-trust" member can recommend winners.
  5. Announce winners; all entrants are notified.
  6. Pay winners to their Earn wallet. Superteam- and Solana-funded listings use a payment form plus KYC, paid within 7 days.
  - Free, no trial needed, no card.
  - Sources: https://superteam.fun/earn/sponsor/ ; https://docs.superteam.fun/the-superteam-handbook/community/faqs/superteam-earn-faq
- **Pricing:**
  - 0% commission and no listing fees (sponsor page).
  - The published rate card sets expectations: X thread $500–1,500, long-form essay $500–1,000, hype video $1,000–2,000.
  - Pro listing terms: unverified; the page shows listings only, no terms.
  - Checked 2026-10-12.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - Fast, permissionless sponsor setup, with no fee or commission.
  - Distribution: 150K Solana freelancers and over 50,000 monthly visitors claimed. Jupiter cites 3,000+ submissions and $300k+ paid out (sponsor page; self-reported).
  - The rate card per content type anchors fair pricing for sponsors.
  - An agent-ready API with AGENT_ALLOWED and AGENT_ONLY listing flags; humans claim and KYC for payout (agents page).
- **What it lacks vs JamJam full build plan:**
  - No brand intake, kit, voice or claims gate (B1–B6, R1–R2).
  - Competitive bounties mean many people do unpaid work. JamJam pays per approved piece instead (Y1).
  - No FTC disclosure insertion and no compliance record (Y2).
  - No onchain record of what was approved or paid. A third-party audit could not see winner payments on public routes, and the Terms disclaim liability if a sponsor doesn't pay (Shaduf walkthrough, Oct 2026) (P2, P3, Y3).
  - Rate card includes paid X threads (Y3 contrast).
- **What JamJam should steal / learn:**
  - Publish a rate card per content format (blog, tutorial, docs page, LinkedIn post) to anchor Y1 payouts.
  - A "post in under 2 minutes" promise for the brief.
  - Use the Superteam network as a supply source: JamJam could pull vetted Earn talent into invited ambassador seats.
  - Its weakness is an opening for JamJam: provable payment receipts (USDC tx plus attestation) solve the "was I ever paid?" gap.
- **Coverage vs loop:** Build: No · Run: No · Prove: No · Pay: Yes (manual sponsor payout)

### Wallchain Select (https://wallchain.xyz)
- **Category:** Former InfoFi leaderboard (Quacks), now managed creator and ambassador campaigns.
- **Status (as of 2026-10-12):** Live. X API access was reported revoked on Jan 15, 2026. Wallchain kept paying out leaderboard campaigns ($1.3M in 2026 by March), then launched Select on May 26, 2026. The homepage now leads with "Ambassador & Growth Partnerships".
- **What it does (2-3 sentences):** The brand supplies its ideal customer profile (ICP), or a competitor or token-holder list. Wallchain matches creators whose audiences overlap, using what it says is an audience map of 3.3M+ X creators. Wallchain then runs sourcing, contracts, briefs, posting, payouts and reporting, and pays creators fixed terms when the campaign closes.
- **Target customer:** Crypto projects, typically pre-TGE or early-stage. Observed Select budgets ran $10k–$30k (Matrica feed).
- **Onboarding flow (step by step, as documented):**
  1. Contact Wallchain via Telegram (@surapyk per docs) or the site's contact.
  2. Share the ICP or look-alike source.
  3. Wallchain returns a creator shortlist.
  4. The brief is agreed. Wallchain says it launches a campaign only once creator rewards are secured.
  5. Creators apply at app.wallchain.xyz/campaigns, using X login.
  6. Creators write the posts in their own words.
  7. Payout and report at campaign close.
  - No self-serve and no trial.
  - Sources: https://news.wallchain.xyz/p/wallchain-select-high-roi-targeted-creator-campaigns ; https://docs.wallchain.xyz/application
- **Pricing:** Unverified; no public price list, sales-led. Campaign budgets seen were $10,000–$30,000 (https://matrica.io/community/wallchain). Checked 2026-10-12.
- **What it does well (specific, evidence-backed):**
  - Look-alike audience matching, which favours niche audience fit over follower count.
  - Fixed terms and funding secured upfront remove the counterparty risk that hurt earlier campaigns.

## Segment takeaways

- **X's Jan 15, 2026 ban split the segment in two.** Survivors either went curated and sales-led (Kaito Studio, Wallchain Select) or moved verification onchain (Layer3, Earnout). Open pay-per-post leaderboards on X are gone (Cookie Snaps) or pivoting (Xeet). JamJam's Y2 (curated) plus Y3 (off-X, per verified piece) matches where the market moved, and avoids the X dependency that Kaito resolved only through a data deal.
- **Nobody in this segment does Build or Prove-of-content.** Every competitor proves an action (task done, user retained, conversion), not that a message is official and approved. JamJam's brand kit, claims gate and SAS-registered approvals (B, R2, P1–P3) have no equivalent here.
- **Pay is commoditised, and the price anchors are public:**
  - Galxe Business+ is $999–$1,699 a month (annual billing).
  - Zealy is $149–$549 a month plus usage credits.
  - Superteam Earn charges 0% commission.
  - Kaito Katalyst charges no service fee on TGE campaigns but diverts 20% of the pool to stakers.
  - A JamJam fee on USDC paid out has to beat Superteam's 0% on value, and the justification is the compliance record plus proof.
- **Locking the budget upfront is now table stakes.** Zealy locks USDC when a quest is created, Wallchain launches only once funded, Kaito Katalyst takes a refundable deposit, and Earnout escrows the budget in a program. Y1's "capped budget" should be an escrowed USDC budget with automatic refunds.
- **Payout proof is a real gap.** On Superteam Earn, sponsors pay off-platform, and a third-party audit could not see winner payments publicly. A USDC transaction linked to an approval attestation would be a visible upgrade for ambassadors.
- **Crypto buyers tolerate wallet-based, deposit-based onboarding, but it adds friction.** Galxe requires a 300 USDT/USDC deposit and wallet login, and Layer3 requires a 12-month token stake. JamJam's email sign-in with no wallet (P4) is a differentiator for non-native brands. Keep a stablecoin deposit option for crypto-native brands.
- **Published rate cards and CPMs build trust.** Superteam publishes rates per content type and Kaito publishes CPM figures. JamJam should publish suggested USDC rates per format (blog, tutorial, docs, LinkedIn) at launch.
