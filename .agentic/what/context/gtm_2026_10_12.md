# JamJam go-to-market (2026-10-12)

## 1. What we sell
**JamJam is your brand team, in one app.** It does four jobs ([ADR-002](../decisions/adr_002_purpose_build_run_prove.md), [ADR-003](../decisions/adr_003_superhub_business_model.md)):
- **Strategy without the agency.** It reads your site, asks only what's missing, and drafts purpose, positioning and voice. Every fact is traced to its source.
- **Content that sounds like you and never lies for you.** Posts, carousels and video. Every claim is checked against your evidence, and nothing goes out until you approve it.
- **Everywhere, provably yours.** It publishes to every channel. Your posts, and your ambassadors' approved posts, are recorded on Solana, so anyone can check it's really you.
- **A community that sells for you.** Ambassadors and partners post from your kit as seats in the app. You approve their posts, and each approved post is verified as yours.

Payments are part of the same picture:
- tips in USDC;
- rewards for real content, disclosed and approved and off X;
- payments to KOLs.

## 2. The two problems we lead with
1. **Every founder needs a brand team, and few can afford one.** The alternatives:
   - an agency;
   - doing it yourself every week;
   - an AI writer that needs content you haven't written and sounds like everyone else ("I end up changing 90% of what is generated", Blaze review on Capterra, [research 04](./research/04_brand_hub_landscape.md) §3).
2. **Proof it's really you.** AI made it free to sound like any brand.
   - US consumers reported $3.5B lost to imposters in 2025, nearly $1B of it to business impersonators ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/06/ftc-data-show-people-reported-losing-3-point-5-billion-imposter-scams-2025)).
   - In crypto, impersonation scams rose 1,400% year on year ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/crypto-scams-2026/)).
   - Nothing lets a fan check whether a post, an ambassador or a KOL really speaks for the brand. C2PA metadata gets stripped by platforms ([research 04](./research/04_brand_hub_landscape.md) §5d).

## 4. Distribution
1. **The free kit is the front door.** A founder pastes a site and gets a brand kit for $0.
2. **Verification spreads itself.** Verified posts carry a check-it badge back to JamJam. Every check shows a fan, journalist or exchange the brand's verified page, and the free kit.
3. **Ambassadors bring brands.** Ambassadors join free as seats ([ADR-003](../decisions/adr_003_superhub_business_model.md)). Many run their own projects, and the free plan gives each of them a kit.
4. **Built in public (planned launch tactic).** JamJam builds its own kit and launch posts with JamJam and shows them on its public Ledger.
5. **Crypto and developer channels:** the Colosseum community, Superteam (2,730+ sponsor accounts on Superteam Earn, [Superteam Earn](https://superteam.fun/earn), checked 6 Oct 2026; a reachable list, not buyers), ecosystem Discords, and the founder's developer network.

## 5. Promotions (proposed)
- **Founding 100 brands:** the launch price is locked for 12 months.
- **Colosseum teams:** Team free for 3 months.
- **Verified-ambassador launch:** the first brands get ambassador seats free while they set up their program.
- **Not doing:**
  - lifetime deals, which lock in unprofitable users;
  - card-required trials, which correlate with the worst review scores in [research 10](./research/10_competitor_onboarding_pricing.md) summary item 8 and §5 "Trials" (Taplio 2.1 and Later 1.3 on Trustpilot).

## 6. Pricing (launch values)
| Plan | Includes | Price | Market band ([research 10](./research/10_competitor_onboarding_pricing.md) §6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Build your brand kit; Verify | $0 | Pomelli and Buffer free tiers |
| Solo | One brand, unlimited posts, publishing, verification | $39 / month | Solo AI voice tools $20–49 |
| Team | Seats and approvals for team, partners and ambassadors | $199 / month | Team tools $165–499; Supergrow Teams and Pressmaster Agency both $199 |
| Tips, rewards and KOL payments | USDC paid through JamJam | 3% | Aspire: brand pays 2% plus processing; Superteam Earn: 0% |

The tiers come from ADR-010 ([PR #55](https://github.com/DeveloperAlly/solana-hack/pull/55)). The 3% is justified by the public record of what was approved and paid. Bounty boards give no such record (research 10 §7.3).

## 8. Demand validation
**What exists today:**
- The founder is customer zero for her own brand, JamJam and aDNA.
- No outside customer pays yet, and no design-partner agreement is signed.
- Market signals, all sourced above:
  - founders already pay for single AI writing tools;
  - impersonation losses are large and rising;
  - no competitor in 59 keeps facts with quotes or a public record of approvals (research 10 §7.3).

**Next 30 days, with pass marks:**

| Test | Pass mark |
|---|---|
| 15 interviews with crypto and developer founders | 5 or more say they would pay for Solo or Team |
| Founder-run brands use it weekly | JamJam and aDNA publish from JamJam every week |
| Outside brands build a kit | 10 kits, at least 3 of them outside the founder's own brands |
| Verified ambassador posts | 1 outside brand approves an ambassador post that verifies |
| Paid | 1 brand pays for Solo or Team |

## 10. Risks
- **Platform rules:** paid work stays off X, and X barred apps that reward people for posting ([ADR-002](../decisions/adr_002_purpose_build_run_prove.md)).
- **Copying:** Pressmaster could add citations. We lead with evidence, recorded approvals and verified content from many voices (research 10 §8).
- **Crypto budgets are cyclical,** so the product stays crypto-neutral and the second market is every founder.
- **The 3% fee competes with 0% bounty boards** (research 10 N14).
