# JamJam go-to-market (2026-10-12)

## 1. What we sell, to whom
- **Product:** a brand ambassador program tool covering four steps. It builds the brand kit from the brand's own site, creates approved posts in the brand's voice, records them on Solana so anyone can check what's official, and pays ambassadors in USDC for each verified piece ([ADR-002](../decisions/adr_002_purpose_build_run_prove.md), [ADR-003](../decisions/adr_003_superhub_business_model.md)).
- **First buyers:** crypto and developer-tool brands ([ADR-007](../decisions/adr_007_pitch_positioning.md)). The product copy stays crypto-silent, so the same product sells to any brand later.
- **Why this beachhead:**
  - **Impersonation hurts them most:** crypto impersonation scams rose 1,400% year on year ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/crypto-scams-2026/)).
  - **They are reachable in one place:** 2,730+ sponsor accounts are registered on Superteam Earn, Solana's bounty board ([Superteam Earn](https://superteam.fun/earn), checked 6 Oct 2026). This is a reachable-account signal, not proof of payment: these are account counts, and research 10 found Superteam payments are not publicly visible.
  - **Their old channel closed:** X ended pay-per-post apps in January 2026. Cookie shut down Snaps, Kaito replaced Yaps with curated campaigns, and Wallchain moved to managed campaigns ([research 10](./research/10_competitor_onboarding_pricing.md) §1.7).
  - **They are reachable:** they gather in a few places (Superteam, hackathons, ecosystem Discords), and the founder has worked in this ecosystem (Lilypad, Filecoin Foundation).
- **Why the full stack, not point tools:** each step needs the one before it. You can only pay for work you can prove, and you can only prove what was approved against the brand kit. Of 59 products reviewed, the best cover two of the four steps ([research 10](./research/10_competitor_onboarding_pricing.md) §1). A brand stitching tools together has no single record linking the approval to the payout.

## 3. Distribution
1. **The Verify page is the channel.** Every approved post can carry a "check it" link to jamjam.tech. Each check shows a fan, journalist or exchange a JamJam brand page and the free Build entry point.
2. **Ambassadors become brands.** Ambassadors join free as seats ([ADR-003](../decisions/adr_003_superhub_business_model.md)). Many run their own projects, and the Free plan gives them their own kit.
3. **Build in public.** JamJam's own kit and posts sit on the public Ledger, and our launch posts are made with JamJam.
4. **Ecosystem placement:**
   - the Colosseum community and later accelerator access;
   - Superteam listings;
   - ecosystem grants to fund pilots (each to be confirmed before applying).

## 4. Promotions (proposed)
- **Founding 50 brands:** the launch price is locked for 12 months.
- **Hackathon teams:** Team free for 3 months for projects from this Colosseum cohort.
- **Launch bounty:** a USDC bounty on Superteam Earn for a written tutorial about JamJam, paid through JamJam once payouts ship. This is our own product, used as our own channel.
- **Not doing:**
  - lifetime deals, which lock in unprofitable users;
  - card-required trials, which correlate with the worst review scores in research 10 §1.8 (Taplio 2.1 and Later 1.3 on Trustpilot).

## 5. Pricing (test values)
| Plan | Includes | Test price | Market band (research 10 §6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Build the kit, Verify | $0 | Pomelli and Buffer free tiers |
| Solo | One brand, posts, register and prove | $39 / month | Solo AI voice tools $20–49 |
| Team | Seats, approvals by role, ambassadors | $199 / month | Team tools $165–499; Supergrow Teams and Pressmaster Agency both $199 |
| Payouts | USDC to ambassadors | Ambassador keeps 100%; brand pays 3% on top | Aspire: creator gets 100%, brand pays 2% plus processing; Superteam Earn charges 0% |

Plan tiers: ADR-010 ([PR #55](https://github.com/DeveloperAlly/solana-hack/pull/55)). The 3% fee is above Aspire's 2%. It is justified only if the public payout record is worth it to brands, so it should be tested with wave 1 and 2 partners.

## 6. Forecast (base case; every input is an assumption)
| Input | Day 90 | Day 180 | Day 365 | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paying Solo brands | 10 | 40 | 120 | Assumption: outreach plus Verify-page signups |
| Paying Team brands | 3 | 12 | 40 | Assumption: about 1.5% of the 2,730 Superteam sponsor accounts by day 365 |
| Monthly ambassador budget per Team brand | $1,000 | $2,000 | $3,000 | Assumption; to be tested with design partners |
| Subscription MRR | 10×39 + 3×199 = $987 | 40×39 + 12×199 = $3,948 | 120×39 + 40×199 = $12,640 | Arithmetic |
| Payout fee MRR (3%) | 3×1,000×3% = $90 | 12×2,000×3% = $720 | 40×3,000×3% = $3,600 | Arithmetic |
| **Total MRR** | **$1,077** | **$4,668** | **$16,240** | Arithmetic |

Payouts are designed, not live ([ADR-008](../decisions/adr_008_submission_critical_path.md), proposed). Until they ship, fee revenue is $0 and the totals are subscription only. Conservative and stretch cases scale both brand counts by 0.5 and 2. The model holds no churn, and that is its main weakness.

## 7. Demand validation
**What exists today (honest):**
- No paying customers.
- No signed design-partner agreements.
- The live database holds 0 brands ([STATE.md](../../../STATE.md)).
- The owner's own use (JamJam and aDNA) is the first validation.

**Market signals:**
- Brands already pay for ambassador tooling: ambassador platforms are priced at $520–2,750 a month; 2,730+ Superteam Earn sponsor accounts are a reachable pool, not confirmed payers ([research 10](./research/10_competitor_onboarding_pricing.md) §6).
- Impersonation losses are high: $3.5B in US imposter losses in 2025 ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/06/ftc-data-show-people-reported-losing-3-point-5-billion-imposter-scams-2025)).
- The approval-and-proof gap is open: no competitor in 59 keeps facts with quotes or a public approval record (research 10 §7.3).

**Next 30 days, with pass marks:**

| Test | Pass mark |
|---|---|
| 15 interviews with Superteam sponsors and dev-tool community leads | 5 or more say they would pay for the Team plan |
| Design partners run their kit and first post | 3 kits registered, each first post in under 10 minutes |
| Paid pilot | 1 brand pays for Team, or funds a USDC ambassador budget |
| Verify usage | Checks per registered post measured and published |

## 8. Risks
- **Platform risk:** X policy on paid posts. Payouts are only for off-X work ([ADR-002](../decisions/adr_002_purpose_build_run_prove.md)).
- **Crypto budgets are cyclical,** so the product copy stays crypto-silent and the ICP stays broad.
- **Payout fee vs 0% rivals** (research 10 N14).
- **Copy risk:** Pressmaster could add citations, so we lead with evidence, recorded approvals and public proof (research 10 §8).
