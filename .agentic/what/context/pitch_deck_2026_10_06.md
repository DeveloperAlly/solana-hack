# Pitch deck v2: Waterlily, build your brand, run it, prove it

**Editable deck:** [Waterlily: build, run, prove](https://claude.ai/artifact/4QPRrRAXx8VF4PsKf5qZ8d) (Slides artifact, owner access). This file mirrors its text and speaker notes so the repo holds the pitch. The deck is the source of truth for layout; update both when the words change.

**Look:** pond ink `#10262B`, lily paper `#F5F1E8`, lily-pad green `#3D7A5A`, bloom coral `#E0846B`. Fraunces (display), Instrument Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono (labels). Provisional; rebrand later.

**Story:** AI made it free to sound like any brand, and the old way of paying communities broke. "Official" should be a public record, and payment should follow verification. Waterlily runs the whole loop (build, run, prove, pay), and no one else does ([research 04](./research/04_brand_hub_landscape.md)). The sale leads with crypto and developer brands.
