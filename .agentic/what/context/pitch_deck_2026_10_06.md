# Pitch deck v4: JamJam, build your brand, create your content, prove it

**Editable deck:** [JamJam: build, create, prove](https://claude.ai/artifact/4QPRrRAXx8VF4PsKf5qZ8d) (Slides artifact, owner access). This file mirrors its text and speaker notes so the repo holds the pitch. The deck is the source of truth for layout; update both when the words change.

**Look:** wireframe, matching the site's `wireframe` theme ([`web/src/tokens/themes/wireframe.json`](../../../web/src/tokens/themes/wireframe.json)): white `#ffffff`, ink `#1a1a1a`, greys `#595959` / `#8a8a8a` / `#bdbdbd`, no colour. Arial (body and display, standing in for the site's system font), IBM Plex Mono (labels). Each slide sits in an outlined artboard frame with its frame name above; solid outlines for real things, dashed for placeholders, crossed boxes for screenshots to come; black fill marks the onchain steps and JamJam's own row ([ADR-009](../decisions/adr_009_rename_jamjam.md)).

**Story:** every founder needs a brand team, and the choices are costly, time-consuming or sloppy (owner's premise; not a measured market fact). JamJam is the brand team in one app: it builds the brand from the founder's own site, runs content in that voice, proves what's official and pays the community. The pitch sells the product as it will be built (backlog phases 1 to 5, [PR #55](https://github.com/DeveloperAlly/solana-hack/pull/55)); the demo video shows what runs today. Teardown and answers: [pitch VC teardown](../../../audits/2026-10-12_pitch-vc-teardown.md).
