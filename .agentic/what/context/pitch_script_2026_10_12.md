## 2. Full script (every spoken word)

**1. Cover** · 0:00–0:10 · 26 words

Hi, I'm Ally Haire. This is JamJam: build your brand, run it, prove it. It runs your ambassador program, and records every official post on Solana.

**2. Problem: impersonation** · 0:10–0:28 · 49 words

AI made it free to sound like any brand. Americans reported losing three and a half billion dollars to imposter scams last year, nearly a billion to fake businesses. In crypto, impersonation grew fourteen hundred percent. And when a fan asks, is this really them, there's nowhere to check.

**3. Problem: ambassador programs** · 0:28–0:45 · 45 words

Brands answer with ambassador programs, but those run on trust nobody can check. The brand is legally responsible for what its ambassadors say. A fake post looks exactly like an approved one. And in January, X cut off the apps that paid people to post.

**4. Product** · 0:45–1:09 · 63 words

JamJam runs the whole program in one place. Paste your site and it builds your brand kit, every fact showing its source. You approve it once. Posts are drafted in your voice, claims checked, and nothing goes out without you. Approved posts are recorded on Solana. Next, ambassadors get paid in USDC for each verified piece. The first four steps are live today.

**5. Check a post** · 1:09–1:18 · 25 words

This is the moment that matters. Anyone can paste a post and get a straight answer: official, from this brand kit, and confirmed on Solana.

**6. Why Solana** · 1:18–1:39 · 56 words

Why Solana? Solana's big push this year is stablecoin payments. JamJam adds a new flow: brands paying ambassadors worldwide in USDC, per verified piece, inside a budget cap. Every approved post is a public record that any wallet, exchange or app can check. And these are ordinary businesses, brought onto Solana by signing in with email.

**7. First customers** · 1:39–1:57 · 49 words

We start with crypto and developer brands. Impersonation is up fourteen hundred percent. More than twenty-seven hundred sponsor accounts are registered on Superteam Earn, Solana's bounty board. And X's ban left creator budgets looking for a home. We're starting with aDNA, film.fun and GamersLab, brands we already work with.

**8. Competition** · 1:57–2:14 · 45 words

We checked fifty-nine products. The best do two steps. None runs the whole program. That matters, because you can only pay for work you can prove, and only prove what was approved against the brand kit. Point tools hold one piece. JamJam holds all four.

**9. Business model** · 2:14–2:39 · 67 words

Kits and checking are free. Solo is thirty-nine dollars a month, and Team, with ambassadors, is a hundred and ninety-nine. On payouts, the ambassador keeps everything and the brand pays three percent. Our base case is a hundred and sixty paying brands, about sixteen thousand dollars a month, by the end of year one. And every check sends people to JamJam, where they become the next brands.

**10. Team and close** · 2:39–2:57 · 47 words

I founded Lilypad, a decentralised AI compute network. In 2023 I built the original Waterlily, AI art that paid the artists it learned from. Before that I led developer advocacy for IBM in Asia Pacific. JamJam: build your brand, run it, prove it. Try it at jamjam.tech.
