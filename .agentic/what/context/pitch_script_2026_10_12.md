## 2. Full script (every spoken word)

**1. Cover** · 0:00–0:09 · 23 words

Hi, I'm Ally Haire. Every founder needs a brand team. Almost none can afford one. JamJam is your brand team, in one app.

**2. Problem** · 0:09–0:28 · 52 words

Today a founder has three bad options. Hire an agency, and spend months and money you don't have. Do it yourself, from a blank page, every week. Or use an AI writer that sounds like everyone else. As one user put it: I end up changing ninety percent of what is generated.

**3. Solution** · 0:28–0:50 · 57 words

JamJam does the brand team's job. Paste your website and get your strategy: purpose, positioning and voice, every fact sourced. Then content that sounds like you: posts, carousels and video, with every claim checked, and nothing goes out until you approve it. Publish everywhere, provably yours. And turn your community into your sales team, paid in USDC.

**4. How it works** · 0:50–1:05 · 41 words

Here's why it's fast. JamJam reads before it asks. Whatever it finds on your site, you just confirm. It only asks what's missing: your story, your beliefs, what you can prove. You sign off, and your brand is ready to use.

**5. Why now** · 1:05–1:24 · 51 words

Why now? AI made content free, so trust is the scarce thing. Americans reported losing three and a half billion dollars to imposters last year, nearly a billion of it to fake businesses. And X has banned paying people to post, so brands need an honest way to reward their communities.

**6. Why Solana** · 1:24–1:45 · 57 words

We don't need a blockchain to write your posts. We need one where trust and money move. Every official post goes on an open record that any wallet, exchange or app can check, and that no one can quietly rewrite, including us. And brands pay their community in USDC, anywhere in the world, inside a set budget.

**7. Go to market** · 1:45–2:03 · 48 words

We start where it hurts most: crypto and developer teams. Impersonation scams in crypto grew fourteen hundred percent last year. More than twenty-seven hundred sponsor accounts sit on Superteam Earn, and none of their community tools does brand or content. Then we take it to every small brand.

**8. Competition** · 2:03–2:17 · 37 words

We checked fifty-nine products. The best do two of the four steps, so founders stitch tools together. JamJam is the only one we found that builds the brand, runs it, proves it and pays for the work.

**9. Business model** · 2:17–2:33 · 41 words

Building your brand kit is free. Solo is thirty-nine dollars a month, Team is a hundred and ninety-nine, and we take three percent on community payouts. Every verified post links back to JamJam, so every brand's audience becomes our next customer.

**10. Roadmap** · 2:33–2:41 · 23 words

Here's the road. Now, your brand in minutes. Next, content you approve without editing. Then publishing everywhere, and paying your community. Then scale.

**11. Team and ask** · 2:41–2:59 · 48 words

I founded Lilypad, a decentralised AI compute network, and in 2023 I built the original Waterlily, AI art that paid its artists. I've built developer communities at IBM and the Filecoin Foundation. We're looking for our first ten design partners. JamJam: build your brand, run it, prove it.
