## 3. Full script (every spoken word)

**1. Cover** · 0:00–0:15 · 39 words

I'm Alison Haire. Making and deploying a brand strategy used to take a full team and six months. Now I just walk through JamJam. It builds my brand, writes my content in my voice, and verifiably grows my brand.

**2. Problem** · 0:15–0:33 · 48 words

Before JamJam, I had three options. Do it myself, and lose every week to a blank page. Hire an agency, and burn the runway. Use an AI writer, and sound like everyone else. As one user put it: I end up changing ninety percent of what is generated.

**3. Solution** · 0:33–0:45 · 33 words

With JamJam, I paste my website and get a brand strategy that sounds like me. Then it writes my posts, gets them out everywhere, brings in ambassadors, and gets better with every post.

**4. How it works** · 0:45–0:59 · 37 words

And I can trust it. Other AI tools make things up. JamJam uses my own words, or tells me when it's guessing. It blocks claims I can't back up. And nothing goes out until I approve it.

**5. Why now** · 0:59–1:15 · 43 words

This is the moment. Imposter scams cost Americans three and a half billion dollars last year. X banned apps that pay people to post. AI agents now pay in stablecoins. I need content I can prove, and rewards that go to real work.

**6. Why Solana** · 1:15–1:32 · 46 words

That's why Solana. Fakes are cheap; proof isn't. When I approve a post, I register it on Solana, so my audience can check it's the exact post I approved. And the same rails pay people: USDC tips, ambassador rewards, and agents paying to use my voice.

**7. Solana integration** · 1:32–1:40 · 20 words

I sign in with just an email. No wallet, no seed phrase. JamJam handles Solana for me. Payouts are next.

**8. Go to market** · 1:40–1:54 · 39 words

I'm starting with people like me: web3 founders and creators. In crypto, impersonation scams grew fourteen hundred percent in a year. I'm customer zero: JamJam markets itself, with JamJam. And four brands have already agreed to beta test it.

**9. Competition** · 1:54–2:06 · 31 words

I looked at fifty-nine products. The best do two of the four jobs. None builds my brand, creates my content, proves it and pays my community in one place. JamJam does.

**10. Business model** · 2:06–2:20 · 36 words

My brand kit is free. Once it's running, it's thirty-nine dollars a month, or one ninety-nine for a team, plus three percent on tips and rewards. Every fan who checks one of my posts discovers JamJam.

**11. Roadmap** · 2:20–2:28 · 24 words

Next, I approve posts without touching them, publish everywhere from one place, reward my fans, and get paid when AI agents use my voice.

**12. Team and ask** · 2:28–2:44 · 40 words

I founded Lilypad and built the original Waterlily, AI art that paid its artists. Now I'm building the brand team I needed as a founder. I'm asking for your vote to take JamJam to mainnet. JamJam. Every post provably yours.

## 4. Appendix talking points (Q&A, not spoken in the 3:00)

**A0 Contents.** Signposts the appendix for Q&A: open the section that answers the question.
- Architecture: how the parts fit and where Solana sits
- UI highlights: one screen per value promise
- Onboarding: from a website to an approved kit and a verified first post
- Intelligence: how drafts stay true and on-voice
- Future features: what the team adds next

**A1 System Architecture.** Answers 'is this real and how is it built'.
- One API Worker on Cloudflare; data in Supabase, read and written only by the Worker
- Email sign-in; a server Registrar signs every Solana record, so brands never hold keys
- Every deploy runs Playwright and a registry self-test on devnet
- Payments come from the brand's own wallet and are confirmed on-chain before they show as paid (planned)
- The evaluation harness that gates prompt and model changes is next (backlog X12)

**A2 Brand Sign-Off.** Answers 'what does a founder actually decide'.
- Three gates: purpose, positioning, voice
- Sources sit beside each section: your answers, and facts with their page links (each fact's quote shows once quote display ships)
- Every later post is written from the approved kit

**A3 On-Voice Content.** Answers 'why is this better than an AI writer'.
- Drafts use the approved voice and are prompted to use only kit facts; the claims gate at approval is where evidence is enforced
- Banned AI phrasing is caught in code and forces a rewrite
- Claims are checked against your evidence at the strictness you set; an unsupported claim blocks approval
- The goal is approval without rewriting; today you can edit a draft before you approve it

**A4 Ambassador Seats.** Answers 'what are ambassadors for'.
- Ambassadors are seats on the brand's account, writing from its kit
- Off-brand claims and missing disclosures are caught before approval
- Approved ambassador posts verify as the brand's, which is the point: verified content is the reason for ambassador seats; payment comes second

**A5 Post Verification.** Answers 'how does a fan tell real from fake'.
- Paste a post or a link
- Official only when the text matches a post the brand approved and registered
- The brand's verified accounts sit beside the result, so a copy from another account stands out (verified accounts are next)

**A6 Public Ledger.** Answers 'why should anyone trust JamJam's record'.
- The latest registered kits and posts, each with a Solana transaction anyone can open
- Only hashes and ids go on-chain; the words stay private
- No one, including JamJam, can quietly rewrite history

**A7 Onboarding Journey.** Answers 'how long until I get value'.
- Read your site and sources; each fact kept with its quote
- Sign in with email to save
- Confirm what it found, answer six short questions, pick a voice template and tune it
- Approve the kit, get a first post, register it
- Today you sign in first, then add sources; reading the site before sign-in is a proposed change

**A8 Intelligence Pipeline.** Answers 'how do you stop the AI inventing things'.
- Specialist agents share one evidence store
- Drafters cite kept facts; a person approves at every gate
- Gap analysis, best of 3 and edit-to-rule learning are next
- Every post: voice draft, anti-slop pass, claims gate, your approval, then the registry

**A9 Voice Learning Ladder.** Answers 'what's the moat'.
- Today: 12 voice templates on 9 dimensions
- Next: your own voice from your posts and your edits
- Later: a shared anti-slop writer trained on approvals, with consent; per-brand models at scale
- Today the drafting model scores its own voice fit; the goal is that nothing grades itself, with independent release gates on every change once the evaluation harness ships (backlog X12, next)

**A10 No AI Slop.** Answers 'how do you guarantee good copy', with the research behind each step.
- Built: kit-facts-only prompt, banned list in the prompt, code check with forced rewrite, claims gate at approval, human approval
- Grounding cuts hallucination (Shuster et al., 2021)
- Slop patterns are measurable (Paech et al., 2025)
- Next: a reviewer from a different model family, because models favour their own output (Panickssery et al., 2024)

**A11 Future Features.** Answers 'what's next and in what order'.
- Create: carousels and video, direct publishing, unified inbox
- Grow: ambassador campaigns, analytics, verified AI persona
- Earn: USDC tips, voice licensing, stablecoin plans
