## 2. Full script (every spoken word)

**1. Cover** · 0:00–0:09 · 24 words

Hi, I'm Ally Haire. Building a brand takes a team. JamJam is your brand team, in one app, and every post is provably yours.

**2. Problem** · 0:09–0:21 · 32 words

Agencies cost too much. AI writers sound like everyone else. One user said: I end up changing ninety percent of what is generated. So you end up doing it yourself, every week.

**3. Solution** · 0:21–0:40 · 52 words

JamJam is the brand team you need: affordable, always available, and verifiably on-brand. It defines your brand from everything you've published, or from scratch, every fact sourced. It writes content that sounds like you, every claim backed. It keeps your team and ambassadors on-brand. And it proves your work is really yours.

**4. How it works** · 0:40–0:54 · 36 words

No blank page. JamJam builds your brand from what you already have: your site, socials, docs, decks and repos, or a short interview if you're starting from scratch. You confirm what it finds, and sign off.

**5. Why now** · 0:54–1:10 · 44 words

Why now? AI lets anyone sound like you, so now you need proof it's really you. Americans reported losing three and a half billion dollars to imposters last year, a billion to fake businesses. And X has barred apps that reward people for posting.

**6. Why Solana** · 1:10–1:38 · 72 words

We don't need a blockchain to write your posts. We need one where trust and money move. Prove you own your domain and accounts once. Then your posts, ambassadors' posts and AI persona go on an open record any wallet or exchange can check, so a fake stands out. Fans tip creators in USDC, and brands reward real content, disclosed and approved. And you get paid when AI agents use your voice.

**7. Solana integration** · 1:38–1:44 · 17 words

Today your kit and posts are on Solana, with email sign-in. Next: verified accounts and USDC payouts.

**8. Go to market** · 1:44–1:57 · 35 words

Our first customers are web3 founders and creators, where impersonation is up fourteen hundred percent. Then everyone who is their own brand. They already stitch together three or four paid tools. Customer zero is me.

**9. Competition** · 1:57–2:09 · 32 words

We checked fifty-nine products. The best do two of the four steps. JamJam is the only one we found that builds the brand, runs it, proves it and pays for the work.

**10. Business model** · 2:09–2:26 · 46 words

Building your brand kit is free. Solo is thirty-nine dollars a month, Team is a hundred and ninety-nine, and we take three percent on tips and rewards. Every verified post carries a badge back to JamJam. Refer a friend, and you both get two months free.

**11. Roadmap** · 2:26–2:33 · 19 words

Next: content you approve unedited, publishing everywhere, tips and fan rewards, then getting paid when agents use your voice.

**12. Team and ask** · 2:33–2:48 · 40 words

I founded Lilypad, a decentralised AI compute network, and built the original Waterlily, AI art that paid its artists. I've built developer communities at IBM and the Filecoin Foundation. JamJam: your brand team, in one app. Every post provably yours.
