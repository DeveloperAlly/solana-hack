## 2. Full script (every spoken word)

**1. Cover** · 0:00–0:11 · 29 words

Hi, I'm Ally Haire. Every founder needs a brand team. Almost none can afford one. JamJam is your brand team, in one app, and every post is provably yours.

**2. Problem** · 0:11–0:28 · 47 words

Every founder needs a brand team. Doing it yourself eats every week. An agency costs a budget you don't have. No tool does the whole job, so you stitch them together. And AI writers sound like everyone else: one user changes ninety percent of what is generated.

**3. Solution** · 0:28–0:48 · 51 words

JamJam is the brand team you need: available, affordable, streamlined and on-brand. It defines your brand from everything you've published, or from scratch, every fact sourced. It writes content that sounds like you, every claim backed. It keeps your team and ambassadors on-brand. And it proves your work is really yours.

**4. How it works** · 0:48–1:00 · 33 words

Six steps, one app. Build your brand and voice from sourced facts. Create content, every claim checked. Distribute everywhere. Verify anywhere, on Solana. Grow your reach with ambassadors. And learn from what works.

**5. Why now** · 1:00–1:14 · 37 words

Why now? Fakes cost Americans three and a half billion dollars last year. X cut apps that pay people to post. AI agents can now pay per request, in stablecoins. And AI copy still reads like AI.

**6. Why Solana** · 1:14–1:41 · 72 words

We don't need a blockchain to write your posts. We need one where trust and money move. Prove you own your domain and accounts once. Then your posts, ambassadors' posts and AI persona go on an open record any wallet or exchange can check, so a fake stands out. Fans tip creators in USDC, and brands reward real content, disclosed and approved. And you get paid when AI agents use your voice.

**7. Solana integration** · 1:41–1:47 · 17 words

Today the Solana registry is live on devnet, with email sign-in. Next: verified accounts and USDC payouts.

**8. Go to market** · 1:47–2:02 · 38 words

Our first customers are web3 founders and creators, where impersonation is up fourteen hundred percent. Then everyone who is their own brand. Comparable AI writing tools list at twenty to forty-nine dollars a month. Customer zero is me.

**9. Competition** · 2:02–2:16 · 39 words

We checked fifty-nine products. The best do two of the four steps. JamJam is the only one we found that builds the brand, creates the content, proves it and pays for the work, and it exports to your tools.

**10. Business model** · 2:16–2:34 · 48 words

Building your brand kit is free. Solo is thirty-nine dollars a month, Team is a hundred and ninety-nine, and we take three percent on tips and rewards. Every check of a post happens on a public JamJam page. Refer a friend, and you both get two months free.

**11. Roadmap** · 2:34–2:44 · 25 words

Next: content you approve unedited, publishing direct to X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads, tips and fan rewards, then getting paid when agents use your voice.

**12. Team and ask** · 2:44–2:58 · 40 words

I founded Lilypad, a decentralised AI compute network, and built the original Waterlily, AI art that paid its artists. I've built developer communities at IBM and the Filecoin Foundation. JamJam: your brand team, in one app. Every post provably yours.
