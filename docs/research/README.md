# Waterlily research (2026-10-06)

Five research tracks commissioned to refine Waterlily's purpose. Each doc cites its sources inline and in a Sources list, and flags where evidence is thin.

| # | Doc | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-brand-pillars.md](./01-brand-pillars.md) | What are the pillars of building a company or personal brand, and what workflow (including a zero-data cold start) should the product take people through? |
| 2 | [02-brand-voice-elements.md](./02-brand-voice-elements.md) | What does a brand voice actually include (beyond tone), and what should a Brand Kit data model capture? |
| 3 | [03-platform-performance.md](./03-platform-performance.md) | What makes a post perform on each platform (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Substack, blogs/SEO)? |
| 4 | [04-brand-hub-landscape.md](./04-brand-hub-landscape.md) | Who else does brand hubs, social ops, replies, listening, lead finding, AI personas and crypto community, and where are the gaps? |
| 5 | [05-lilypad-quest-api.md](./05-lilypad-quest-api.md) | Can the Lilypad quest API power Waterlily community quests? |
